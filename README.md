# Six Steps to Freedom — English contents

English translations of the Chinese documents in this repository, in the same order as the original Chinese contents page, [chinese/README.md](chinese/README.md) (中文目录). Original repository: [deng-bowen/6-steps](https://github.com/deng-bowen/6-steps).
Translations are by Claude (Anthropic, model Opus 5.5), November 2026. The Chinese originals, with their pictures, are kept unchanged in the [chinese](chinese) folder, and each English document links to its original at the top.
Where a document already contained English — Lester Levenson’s own words, or Kam Bakhshi’s letter — that English is kept exactly as it was and shown alongside the new translation.
**Progress: 72 of 72 documents translated.**


A note on names: **Feng** (风) is the teacher whose talks make up most of this collection; his name means “Wind”. “The Release Method” (释放法) is the Sedona Method as originally taught by Lester Levenson.
