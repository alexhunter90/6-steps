# A Letter from Kam Bakhshi

> **About this document.** Kam Bakhshi, an Englishman, wrote this letter in English after going free through the Sedona Method. The original file has two parts: (1) a transcription of his English letter, titled “Image version” because it was copied from photographs of the letter, and (2) a Chinese section with translations and commentary. Part 1 below is kept exactly as it appears in the repository, including its original spelling slips. Part 2 is Claude's translation of the Chinese section.

## Part 1 — Kam Bakhshi's letter in his own English (image version, unchanged)

Dear Graduates:

I have been releasing for about 4 years. I felt that I had reached a peaceful place of being: little did I realize that I had just been floating above my garbage.

Just under a year ago, I found out about Larry and The Abundance Course and gave hem a call. In the past I had reached states of being sometimes for as long as a year, but rubbish would still resurface. Larry explained that I had been in a nice space but had been just floating above my garbage. I could quite easily have stayed in that space this whole lifetime, however little growth would have been made.

He explained that it was a very common pitfall of the method. You reach a nice quiet place stay there, but do not get rid of the remaining garbage. I have so much gratitude towards Larry for pointing that out to me and enabling me to continue on the journey towards freedom.

I ordered the Abundance Course and began working with tapes.

The things that Lester has spoken about started to become apparent. Larry was kind enough to word with me and started to point out to me that I was experiencing things that Lester had spoken about. Things started to open up for me. I decided to fly from England to Los Angeles to teke a Live Course and spend some time with Larry. I also ordered the Goals and Resistance course and all of the Advanced Courses.

I would like to share some of the things that have happened to encourage you to continue releasing for freedom.

The mind stilled. So what you might say. But let me expain: no more worries, doubts, recriminations, etc. Just a delightful peace. A deep sense of resting no matter what was happening. No matter what happens now there is always taht state of resting. Of being. The mind can never touch me again. It comes and goes but I know it can never touch me again.

Kam comes and goes, and that's ok too. There is deep sense of rest like in deep sleep. It is so beautiful and peaceful. This deep, deep sense of peace. It really has to be experienced to be believed.

The world is seen for what it is. A dream or drama. The drama goes on. I just watch, like sitting in a movie theater. Everything is ok. There is no need to change anything because everything is as it should be. Everything is perfect.

You start to see everybody as part of you. There is no other. There never was any other. There is only one and we are that. There is a sense of "coming back" to play the game. But this time just for having fun. There is only beingness.

Lester and all the masters are here with us. Every single second they are with us. We just have to call on them. Now there is just a resting. Things just happen, without effort.

I would encourage every one of you who has the method to continue releasing. To continue releasing until you have all the peace and joy that is rightfully yours. And this is possible this very lifetime if you would do it.

We start and then we stop. Then we start again, and then we stop. And so it goes on. This is because the decision has not been made. Once the decision has been made then the releasing momentum starts, and releasing becomes easier and easier.

The problem is you do not want it. You talk about it and think about it, but ultimately you really do not want it. If you did then there would be no stopping.

You would rather bathe in misery. You love your stories and suffering too much. Take a good look inside and see for yourself.

The method is the most powerful tool available. It is the most highest method possible. What it is? "I am not this feeling. I am not this feeling. I am not this feeling." It is stopping you indentifying with the lesser part of you.

So make the decision now, the peace and relief you will experience is ecstasy.

Love, Kam Bakhshi

Kam Bakhshi, United Kingdom

## Part 2 — Claude's translation of the Chinese section

> **About this part.** The Chinese section of the original file is not a translation of the letter above. It is a Chinese blog post by Xu Yaoren (许耀仁), who translated an English forum post by someone called “tom”. Tom quotes from a *different* letter Kam wrote to him personally. Some of its paragraphs match the letter above closely; others (especially the practical advice on the Six Steps near the end) appear only here. Tom's original English isn't in this repository, so everything below is translated from the Chinese. Read it as a faithful rendering of the Chinese, not as Kam's or Tom's exact words. Where a passage matches Kam's English in Part 1, his own wording there is the authority.

Translated by Xu Yaoren

Title: Reaching Freedom

Posted by: tom on April 01, 2008, 02:01:17 AM

There always seem to be people who doubt the Sedona Method because, apart from Lester, they haven't heard of anyone who reached liberation through it. So they want to know whether anyone has reached the same state by learning the principles Lester taught. The biggest problem in finding out whether others have gone free through releasing is this: once those people realise true freedom, they enter true bliss, and they no longer need to be the Doer.

When you awaken, you don't automatically become a teacher, a master, or someone eager to teach others what you've found. In fact the opposite is more logical: you are in true joy, and the only thing you want is to stay in that state.

To teach others, you would have to lower yourself to their level in order to teach them effectively. From what I've read about Lester, he withdrew and enjoyed that true joy for many, many, many years before he decided to start teaching. I think most people, once awakened, would rather stay invisible than become teachers. So there are probably many people who have gone free through Lester's method — we just don't know about them.

I do, however, personally know a man who reached liberation through the Sedona Method in 2003. He's English, and his name is Kam Bakhshi. I'd like to share some passages from the letter he wrote to me after he went free:

“I'd like to share with you some of the things that have happened to me, to encourage you to keep releasing all the way to liberation.

The mind became still. ‘So what?’ you might say. Let me explain: no more worry, doubt, self-blame and so on — just a delightful peace. A deep rest, whatever happens. Whatever is happening now, I remain in that rest. A pure state of being. The mind can no longer affect me. It comes and goes, but I know it can never affect me again.

Kam comes and goes (translator's note: meaning his small self, the ego), and that's fine too. I am in a deep rest, as in deep sleep — so beautiful, so peaceful. You really have to experience it to believe it.

I see the physical world for what it really is: a dream, or a play. The drama goes on, and I just watch, as if sitting in a cinema. Everything is OK. There is no need to change anything, because everything is appearing exactly as it should. Everything is perfect.

You begin to see everyone as a part of yourself. There is no one else in the world, and there never was anyone else. From beginning to end there is only One, and we are that One. There is no longer any duality. Nothing has ever happened, and everything is happening. There is a sense of ‘coming back’ to carry on with the game — but this time purely for fun.

I do nothing, and I have never done anything. There is only Beingness. Lester and every master throughout the ages have always been with us, every minute and every second. We only need to call on them.

Now there is only rest. Things may happen or may not happen, but none of it has anything to do with me — and all of it has everything to do with me.

I encourage every one of you who has this method to keep releasing. We always do it for a while and then stop, start again for a while and stop again, start again and stop again, endlessly.

The reason is that the decision hasn't been made. Once you make the decision, the momentum of releasing starts to build, and releasing gets easier and easier.

The biggest problem is that you don't really want to be free. You may like talking and thinking about freedom, but honestly, you don't really want it. If you really wanted it, nothing could stop you. The truth is that you'd rather keep wallowing in misery; you love the stories you've made up, and your suffering, far too much. Take a good look inside and see for yourself whether this is so.

See clearly what the mind actually is, and stop foolishly treating yourself and the garbage as one and the same. Let it tell its stories — they have nothing at all to do with you.

The peace and inner release you will experience are hard to put into words. It is a lasting peace. The physical world still has its situations and dramas, but the peace is always there.

I ask you to keep releasing. Along the way, love yourself a little more and be a little gentler with yourself. You are the same as me — there is no difference at all. I'm sharing this with you to encourage you to go all the way, and to end all suffering once and for all.”

He also wrote:

“Go over the ‘Six Steps’ often. They contain everything you need to know. They're like a map to liberation, and they keep you on the right path. Everything you need to know intellectually is contained in the Six Steps.

Make absolutely sure you first achieve Step One: you must want freedom more than you want the world. If you can do Step One, nothing can stop you. Please make sure you meet Step One.

Step Two is to make the decision to go free. Going free takes only one decision. As an infinite being, of course you can decide at any moment to drop all of this.

Talk less. Try to become quiet and stay quiet. Every time you want to open your mouth, see your wanting approval, wanting control and wanting security, and release it.

Watch less television, listen to the radio and read newspapers less. Instead, see that you want to escape through these things, and release.

Eat as lightly as you can. We often use food to suppress feelings.

Give up drugs and alcohol as far as you can, because these too are things we often use to suppress feelings.

Step Four of the Sedona Method is to release constantly, and for me this step is the key. Try to spend more than 50 per cent of your waking time releasing. That means you spend more time being your Self than not being your Self. If you do this, the mind falls away. If you do this, you start to find it strange to live inside your head, and you rest more and more often as your Self.

Remember, you are already totally free.”
