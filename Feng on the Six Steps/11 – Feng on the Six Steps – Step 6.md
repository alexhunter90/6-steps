# Feng on the Six Steps: Step 6

**Step Six:** Each time you release, you are happier, lighter and freer of limitation. As you go on releasing, you become happier and happier, lighter and lighter, and freer and freer of limitation.

> **Lester's original English, as given in the original:** Each time you release,you are happier and lighter.You have more motivation to go free.

## [24 October 2020 — Check how releasing feels against Step Six: every release brings lightness and joy]

**Feng:** If every release brings you lightness and joy, then you're releasing. If your releasing stops, that's being stuck.

Keep releasing your feelings, and keep getting lighter and happier.

## [22 October 2020 — Release the feelings covering your true nature, and you'll naturally feel joy]

**Feng:** You can use Step Six to check your releasing at any time. Once you feel light and joyful, keep releasing wanting approval and control.

Because your natural true nature is very joyful, whenever there's no joy, something is still covering it. That's easy to understand.

## [22 October 2020 — The joy and freedom each release brings make you naturally want to keep releasing]

Releasing without getting stuck means that, every time you release, you're lighter, happier and freer. Use Step Six to verify your releasing. If you feel you're fighting a long, drawn-out war with it, you're bogged down. If releasing feels like hard work, you're bogged down.

You must watch out for this. Releasing isn't a war of attrition. I know “releasing constantly” sounds like one, but it really isn't. Every single release brings you enormous joy and freedom, and then you naturally want to keep releasing.

**[23 October 2020 — Every release makes you lighter and happier]**

**Feng:** Step Six means that every release makes you lighter and happier. Note: it's joy brought by releasing, not joy brought by some other, limiting means.

**Value Step Six.**
**The lightness and joy releasing brings will greatly spur Step One.**

## [18 October 2020 — The sense of freedom Step Six brings strengthens the wanting freedom of Step One]

**Feng:** Step Six is also the step we most easily overlook.

Each release makes you freer. Of course it's also described as lighter and happier, but in the end it all comes down to freedom.

The momentum these experiences bring strengthens your wanting freedom in Step One. And Lester said, “If you fully do Step One, you will naturally carry out the other five steps.”

## [20 November 2020 — Use Step Six to spur Step One, and gradually turn around]

**Tiancheng:** How did you really achieve Step One?

**Feng:** Follow the Six Steps all the way through. Use the *gain* of Step Six to spur Step One. It's a perfect process.

Normally you look for happiness everywhere in the world, and that forms your main goals. But when you gain things through releasing, you discover more and more the source of happiness, until you find that the only source of happiness is the infinite being within you. Then you lose all your outer goals, your whole direction turns around, and you reach Step One. When you reach Step One, you fall into the last five steps.

## [22 October 2020 — Reminding yourself of Step Six with each release greatly speeds up your releasing]

**Feng:** Wanting freedom needs momentum. Every time you remind yourself of Step Six, it gives your Step One a push, at a very basic, subtle level. This will greatly speed up your releasing, because the later steps then get carried out naturally.

The key word in Step Six is “more”. Every release makes you happier, lighter and freer.

Notice that it's *every* release. If you get stuck even once, you won't want to release. The momentum of avoidance will quickly pull you in the opposite direction.

Remember what I said a few days ago? The whole mind is wanting, so until you're free, you'll always have wants. The difference is *how to direct it*: whether you direct it towards freedom or towards limitation.

Wanting freedom points you towards continuing to release. That is, you do it once, and you know that next time you'll feel better and experience more freedom, so naturally you want to do it a second time.

After you feel joy, keep releasing, and you'll feel even more joyful and freer. Then release again, and it will soar even higher. The feeling keeps soaring upward. That unbroken joy gives you the momentum to keep releasing.

## [23 October 2020 — What if releasing doesn't feel joyful?]

**I Am Infinite:** How do I do Step Six?

**Feng:** Every release brings lightness and joy.

**I Am Infinite:** I can't release at all, so there's definitely no joy.

**Feng:** If you remind yourself of this (Step Six), you'll stop what you're doing and lightly allow the wanting control to surface.

Whenever you use effort, that's wanting control. The joy will get stuck, because you want to control it.

It seems a lot of people don't know how to do Step Five, so let me share in detail. If you follow the original process, you'll find the first step is “relax as much as you can and bring your attention inside”, and, with your body relaxed and comfortable, become aware of what you're feeling right now.

How to actually use Step Five really is easy to misunderstand. Because people often find they can't feel the wanting to control their feelings, and then, when they try to release the wanting to control the wanting control, they get stuck endlessly. So relax, and become aware of the “want” that's most on the surface. You only need to be aware of one “want” at a time.

When you're stuck, obviously you want to change it. Notice that you're forcing — this “forcing” is wanting control, and it's right on the surface, so there's no need to dig for it deliberately.

Control is the only ingredient of being stuck.

Another point is that, like all feelings, it isn't released all at once. So when I'm guiding people, I say, “Do you still want to change your present feeling?” Allow more of the wanting to change to be noticed. When you've successfully released once the wanting to change that was keeping you stuck, feel again whether there's any left, and release it a second time.

Keep going until you've completely released your wanting to change it, and you'll immediately find the releasing process becomes very smooth. If you only release a little and push the rest back down, then carry on releasing other subjects with this wanting control still there, the wanting control you didn't finish releasing will stay inside you, running you, and you'll get stuck again in some later release.

This was also my biggest obstacle when I first started releasing constantly. In fact, while releasing, we really do just overlook it. So, once more: use the Six Steps. The Six Steps are the only key. They're not just a theory; they're a map that carries you all the way.

## [19 September 2020 — Real releasing brings joy every time]

**Feng:** The feeling of releasing is that the feeling covering things leaves clearly, and then a wave of joy wells up from underneath. That's one release. If that doesn't happen, it may just be your brain's imagination.

**Suile:** I've never had a single feeling of joy.

**Feng:** Mm. Then you've found where the problem lies. You must learn to release first before you can talk about releasing constantly. Real releasing is that every time you become more joyful.

**Suile:** Am I releasing wrongly?

**Feng:** From my own experience, I'd say so. Because once you've experienced releasing even once, you won't confuse it with anything else.

**Suile:** Probably wrong, then. What should I do?

**Feng:** The Release Method isn't complicated. It's just *let up and let go* (let the feeling come up, let the feeling go). But many people, in actual practice, have never experienced releasing; they take some imagining in the head for releasing.

When you release, try not to modify the method; that will only weaken its power. Don't mix and match, don't modify it. Try it like that, and see whether you can find the feeling.

Releasing is a very clear feeling. When you release once, you'll find the experience is obviously different from suppressing or escaping, so you won't confuse them.

**Suile:** Where's my problem?

**Feng:** I think you're suppressing emotions — habitually suppressing. When I was practising *The Abundance Book*, I habitually suppressed all the time. Because I saw the tube method and misunderstood it, I imagined a tube and tried hard to squeeze the feeling out.

Then, also because of a line I read in the book, I experienced releasing for the first time. It was “the feeling itself also wants to get out”, and “decompressing” (relieving the pressure). The feeling is pushing itself out; as long as you don't hold it down and you let go, it goes out by itself.

That experience overturned my understanding of releasing. After that, releasing was very light, and very clear. Try to experience it more.

For me, reciting the name of the Lord a hundred times didn't give me as stunning an experience as one clear release.

So the key is how to release, and how to release constantly.

As for the truth, if you release constantly you'll see it. There's no need to keep studying it in words. However much you study, it's still only words.

(Note: Only part of this conversation is excerpted here, mainly to remind everyone to take their own releasing seriously — don't take not releasing for releasing, and don't forget to check your releasing with Step Six. Only by honestly facing the results of your releasing can you move forward better. The danger isn't not knowing how to release; it's not knowing that you don't know, and taking suppressing for releasing.)

## [21 October 2020 — Keep releasing your feelings, and keep getting lighter and happier]

**Question:** How do I keep releasing when I'm feeling joy?

**Feng:** Become aware of what you want to do with the joy you feel right now. Do you want to keep this joy? Or do you want to change it, to make it even more joyful?

When you release this wanting control, your feeling will *move up*, and you'll find the joy you just had was only relative; there are better feelings ahead. Then you keep releasing, and it feels better and better. So don't stop there — head for ultimate freedom.

## [3 December 2020 — Constant releasing can't be achieved by forcing; it is built on the lightness and joy Step Six brings]

**Feng:** You can set a number of releases, but be careful: if you push too hard, you'll get stuck.

You can't do it with that kind of forcing. The constancy of the Six Steps is built on feeling lighter after you release.

Keep the Six Steps firmly in mind and use them to correct your releasing.

## [17 November 2020 — You can't force yourself to release; you release only when you see what it does for you, so keep confirming your gains and your direction]

> **Translator's note:** This talk and the chat with Star of Bethlehem (A-Heng) are the same as in [Feng's Talks on the Six Steps (3)](../The%20Shortcut%20to%20Freedom/04%20%E2%80%93%20Feng%27s%20Talks%20on%20the%20Six%20Steps%20%283%29.md), and the translation matches that one.

**Feng:** Rely on the Six Steps. Start with the Six Steps, finish with the Six Steps. Use your *gain* to confirm your direction.

Constant releasing has become a “task” for you, and that isn't good. You release constantly because every release brings a benefit.

That is what Step Six says: each release is lighter and happier.

You feel good, so you carry on releasing. If you just force yourself to release, you'll stop. You won't be able to keep going, because at the same time you are suppressing your desires for the world.

My way is to keep confirming my *gain*, and my direction.

For example, she was just about to listen to music. What does she want to get from the music? Can she get that from releasing? Why not give it a try?

Constantly confirming your gains is what keeps you releasing constantly. If you say, “I can do it, I must release, I'm not allowed to listen to music”, that won't work, because one of your subconscious directions is listening to music, and you've pushed it down.

Your conscious and subconscious minds are pulling against each other. If you don't look at that, it won't succeed. Write down the things you think might bring you happiness — where you think happiness is, where you actually go looking for happiness.

If you suppress these desires, you can't possibly achieve Step One. You can force yourself to do your homework and your job, but you can't force yourself to release. *(laughs)*

Let me go into Lester's favourite teaching in detail: *get everything by releasing only*.

What do you want? How are you planning to go about it? Why not get it through releasing? That is how you practise it: you choose to release.

Sometimes you might spend a whole afternoon doing other things and not releasing. That's because your subconscious is looking for something. There's no way to force yourself to release.

You need to see what you're looking for, and ask yourself, “Can I get this from releasing?” See which way is lighter, easier and happier, and so choose releasing.

That's why I say experiences of success are important. You have to get a benefit first. Without a benefit you won't do it; the mind will do everything it can to avoid releasing and resist releasing — that's your mind.

I remember saying before that it was valuing Step Six that truly brought me into constant releasing.
