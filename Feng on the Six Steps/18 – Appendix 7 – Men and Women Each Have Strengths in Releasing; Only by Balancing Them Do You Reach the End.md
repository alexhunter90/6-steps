# Appendix 7: Men and Women Each Have Strengths in Releasing; Only by Balancing Them Do You Reach the End

**3 January 2021**

**Tiancheng:** You said before that there are more female masters than male ones. Is that so?

**Feng:** There isn't much difference, and masters have no gender.

**Tiancheng:** Because you rarely see female masters, people seem to think it's hard for women to become enlightened.

**Feng:** Female masters don't have the acceptance in society that male masters have, so they choose to live in seclusion, that's all. Once men and women are equal in people's collective consciousness, female masters will come forward.

**Tiancheng:** Actually, isn't it easier for women to become enlightened than men? Because women are more in touch with feeling.

**Feng:** Men and women each have their strengths. Women's advantage is at the start, because they operate by feeling. Men's advantage is that their intellectual centre is active, so they have a stronger will to go all the way to the end.

**Tiancheng:** Intellectual centre — what's that?

**Feng:** Between the eyebrows.

**Tiancheng:** The third eye?

**Feng:** Mm. Men usually have a stronger wish to find the answer; women often stop in happiness.

**Tiancheng:** Then what advantage do women have for reaching the end?

**Feng:** Most men never even get started; it's all activity inside their heads. Women go faster; it's just that they easily stop in happiness. Overcome this, balance your characteristics, and you'll reach the end.

For men, overcoming the momentum of their thinking and overcoming their sexual desire is also a way of balancing their characteristics.

The two sexes each have their strengths, but in the end you need to balance.

**Tiancheng:** Of the thirty masters in the world today, how many are women?

**Feng:** I won't say. And it doesn't mean anything anyway: if I made a female body tomorrow, I'd be a female master. The body is only an image that can be transformed.
