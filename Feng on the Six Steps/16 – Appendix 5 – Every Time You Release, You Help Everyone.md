**Question:**
So when Lester left the world, he merged directly into the Infinite?

**Feng:**
I told you that a long time ago.

**Question:**
Later I heard you say that after Lester left, many of his students saw him.

**Feng:**
Only in some form.

**Question:**
You also said that after merging into the Infinite, one doesn't appear in any form.

**Feng:**
Merging into infinite being isn't disappearing; it's existing without form.

**Question:**
Ralph said that the day after Lester left, Lester came to see him. In what form did Lester come?

**Feng:**
In a dream. You can see Lester in any form. Lester is no longer a body. Lester is right beside every one of us now, in the “form” of infinite being.

If you want to see Lester in astral form, you'll see him in astral form. If you want to see Lester in physical form, you'll see Lester in physical form. It depends on your acceptance.

But he is everywhere, at every point. There is nowhere he isn't present at this moment. After merging into the Infinite, he no longer appears in any form of his own.

**Question:**
If my acceptance were great enough, could I still see Lester in physical form?

**Feng:**
Mm. That isn't him “appearing” — he's here now. You just haven't projected his physical form. It isn't that a “Lester” turns up as a physical body; it's that you're trying to communicate with the body of a “Lester”.

If many people around the world projected Lester's body at the same time, they would all see Lester's body at once. But that wouldn't be Lester coming down; it would be the all-powerful working of infinite being.

**Question:**
After merging into the Infinite, Lester is no longer a body but exists in the form of “infinite being”. But if I wanted to see Lester in physical or astral form (and could accept it), I could still see Lester in physical form — is that right?

**Feng:**
Yes. Jesus and the Buddha too. Lester also told his students you could have coffee with Jesus.

**Question:**
Having merged into the Infinite, no longer a body (physical, astral or causal), no longer reincarnating — a *siddha* exists for ever at every point in the universe in the form of “infinite being”, and goes on helping others?

**Feng:**
Mm.

**Question:**
A *siddha* will still go on helping others gain freedom — only in the form of infinite being, not as a body?

**Feng:**
He unconsciously projects everyone, helping them gain freedom. In fact a *siddha's* help is the greatest of all; it is at the level of infinite being. Your mind just can't understand it, so you think teaching in words helps more. In reality, the formless help is the greatest.

**Question:**
The scope of a *siddha's* help is all life and all existence, while a *jivanmukta* only helps a few people?

**Feng:**
A *jivanmukta* also helps all life. The masters are all unconsciously projecting all life, even if they're sitting in a cave. But a *jivanmukta* hasn't finished playing the game of helping people; he still keeps some limitation. A *siddha* is a perfect being.

**Question:**
“A *jivanmukta* also helps all life” — which stage exactly is meant by *jivanmukta* here?

**Feng:**
The first fruit to the fourth.

**Question:**
A stream-entry noble one can help all life too?

**Feng:**
Mm. Not just a stream-entry noble one — every time you release, you are helping all life. The world is only a projection of the mind.

**Question:**
How does that help? For example, if I reached *nirvikalpa samadhi* but my mother doesn't study or practise, can it still help her?

**Feng:**
It helps far more than you sharing things with her would. People don't know how karma works. Releasing makes you more selfless, and as long as you have a selfless consciousness, your mind will project a more perfect world. That is real help.

But in an AGFLAP state, so-called helping others is only gratifying the ego. Feeding the ego doesn't produce much help in itself. Once you're free, even if you sit in a cave doing nothing, you help more than all the activists put together.

**Question:**
Every time I release, I certainly help my own world — and I can help my mother's world too?

**Feng:**
Yes. Every time you release, you help everyone.

**Question:**
Every time I release, I help everyone's world. Everyone means everyone?

**Feng:**
Everyone means everyone.
