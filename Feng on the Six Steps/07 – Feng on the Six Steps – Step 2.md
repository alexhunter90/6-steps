**Step Two:** Make the decision to be free.

> **Lester's original English, as given in the original:** Make the decision to go free.

## [23 October 2020 — If you've really decided, you'll do it straight away]

**Feng:** Deciding on freedom isn't a prayer, and it isn't autosuggestion. A decision is a decision. It's like deciding right now to get up from your seat and walk to the door. Once you've decided, you do it straight away.

## [23 October 2020 — Your decision takes you where you want to go]

**Feng:** Step One is wanting freedom; Step Two is deciding on freedom.

If you've only decided to feel good, or to reach some material goal, you'll stop once you've got it.

If you decide on freedom, you won't stop until you reach freedom.

Because whatever you want and decide on, you'll take yourself there.

## [24 October 2020 — Decide to reach freedom through releasing]

**Feng:** Step Two is simply a decision: decide on freedom, decide to use this releasing process to reach freedom.

Sometimes you hesitate. You think, “Should I release this or not?” Or you want to use another method, such as awareness, or self-enquiry. As long as you haven't decided to use the releasing process to reach freedom, you'll keep wavering like this, and you won't be able to keep going.

Wanting freedom and deciding on freedom are both things you can simply do.

***just want it more, just make the decision.***

When a wanting control or wanting approval is running you, and you're sure that releasing it is better than leaving it inside, it will come up.

For example, say you want to go and play a game right now. There's a wanting control in it, running you. If you feel that releasing this wanting control matters more than controlling, you've achieved Step One, and it will come up for you to release.

Wanting control and approval = wanting the world — that is, going in the direction of limitation. There's no freedom to be had in the direction of limitation.

## [2 January 2021 — Everyone can decide at any moment to reach freedom; this is entirely free will. There is only one thing you have free will about: choosing freedom or bondage]

**Lanruo:**
So Lester's awakening in that life was preset too?

**Feng:**
Awakening is the only thing that can't be preset. About everything else, you have no free will to speak of.

There's only one thing you have free will about, and that's choosing to identify with your body or with infinite being. About anything else at all, there's no free will. Every thought you have is pushed along by past programs. What you want and what you're averse to depend on the tendencies of your programs.

There's only one thing that is entirely free will: at any moment, you can identify with your infinite being.

Releasing is the way.

**FREE:**
Then our coming across the Release Method must have been preset too?

**Feng:**
Of course. Without very, very good karma, you couldn't possibly come across the Release Method. When you reach *nirvikalpa samadhi*, every one of your lives comes up into view. After that you recognise that in everything, it isn't you acting; you have always been infinite being. These later things don't really need saying. I still want to stress the Six Steps: follow them all the way through.

Fully completing Step One needs constant releasing. Pay attention to all of the Six Steps. But if your will power (*will power*) is strong enough, you can also achieve Step One in an instant, through longing alone.

Anyone can be free, as long as you do it.

If you go very fast at the start but stop partway, you can't be free. Follow the Six Steps all the way to the end; following them through is essential.

**Yitian:**
Deciding on freedom is deciding to release. Does deciding to release require first seeing through the fact that the head is the only obstacle to freedom?

**Feng:**
A decision is a decision. Right now you could release, or you could escape your emotions and go and play on your phone — but you decide to release.

When you achieve Step One, you only need to make one decision: decide on freedom. Don't try to break through using the head in any way. Practise along with the videos.

Two years ago I released constantly once, for a week. At that time the AGFLAP in my mind had been mostly cleared out, and then I was in a very comfortable state, and the world around me showed a harmony it had never shown before.

Then for a whole six months I didn't do a single release, and all that AGFLAP came back. I even forgot how to release emotions and wants, and I was stuck all the time. That's why I joined the group to talk things over.

Later I found that people in the group didn't seem to know that Lester's later recordings and the original version of the Release Method existed. So I translated *The Way* and two parts of the 1992 course.

In the course of translating, I went over it all again myself and remembered the key points. So I started releasing constantly again and, remembering the lesson of the last time, went forward in one go.

Before you reach the end, you must not stop. Because as soon as you're not moving forward, you're moving backwards.

Because you haven't completely released wanting control and approval, and the fear of death, the root of the tree hasn't been cut, and the branches and leaves will slowly grow back.

There's no such thing as standing still. As soon as you're not moving forward, you're moving backwards.

“I'll do it later” is a trick of the head. As soon as you use the future tense, your head will keep it in the future.

“Later” means never.

In this age, only very few people tend towards freedom.
