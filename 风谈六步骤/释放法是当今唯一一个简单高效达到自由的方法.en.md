**Question:**
So the Release Method was prepared specifically for this age?

**Feng:**
Yes. Yogananda chose to leave after Lester had fully awakened, and that announced the end of the age of the guru.

Lester could only help a small number of people, and in this age only very few people will awaken. Everyone who has this method has the chance.

There will be many false masters in this age, but in this age only the Release Method can lead to freedom. So anyone who claims to have gone free by another means is a false master.

I mean after 1970. For those awakening after 1970 — apart from those who were already awake in a previous life — only the Release Method can do it.

And anyone who has already awakened by another means will recommend the Release Method to you, whatever method they used themselves, because a master knows everything.

**Question:**
Not even the ultimate method Ramana taught?

**Feng:**
In theory, the ultimate question is the fastest. But in practice, no one on this planet can use it at present.

**Question:**
What about the Kriya Yoga that Yogananda taught?

**Feng:**
You won't go free in this lifetime. Kriya Yoga's demands are too high. It belongs to Raja Yoga — it's a comprehensive path.

The Release Method is the only simple, easy method in this age.

Watch the video above. Lester actually told his students many times that the Sedona Method is the only one that works now, and that most people who don't know the Sedona Method will take a long, long time to reach freedom.

**Question:**
If we don't use it to the utmost, we really are the stupidest people in the world.

**Feng:**
You have no reason not to use the Release Method. Whether for moving towards awakening or getting a better world, it's the most efficient. Not only efficient, but simple.

Because you'll all go through low points, and when you're in a low point it's very easy to give up. If something tempting-looking comes along at that moment, you'll be drawn to it.

So what I'm stressing is something I know: in the present age, starting from 1970, for these 120 years, only the Release Method can take you to the end.

Giving up the Release Method means giving up freedom in this lifetime, whatever other spiritual method you switch to.
