**14 March 2021**

**Question:** Teacher, could you talk about how karma works?

**Feng:** Karma is programs. Programs create every one of your thoughts. Your thinking isn't free, so your actions aren't free either.

**Question:** Teacher, please go on about karma.

**Feng:** Karma is programs — action and reaction.

**Question:** How does it work?

**Feng:** Programs drive thoughts, and thoughts project the world. That's how it works. The world is only a display screen. Whatever you do in the world is just smashing the screen; it can't change any of the story playing on it.

**Question:** So releasing the programs directly can change the world?

**Feng:** Yes. Rather, only releasing the programs can change the world; otherwise the world will play out according to the programs already set. Within the programs you have no free will, unless you decide to release the programs. That's why, whatever schemes you've all wanted to try before, I've always said: go and release. Scheming is useless. It's just being driven by programs, repeating past karma, nothing more.

**Question:** Can I change my family's world through releasing? For example, I want to heal my mother's illness through releasing (even if she preset this illness herself).

**Feng:** Yes.

**Question:** So I can change other people's karma through releasing?

**Feng:** You aren't changing anyone else's karma.

**Question:** Isn't that interfering in someone else's life?

**Feng:** This is the harmonious working of the universe. At this point you're nowhere near interfering with karma; that's something said to people who are free. Because a free person can do anything and can go beyond all the bonds of karma. Right now you're being driven by karma. All your desires are part of karma, so all you affect is your own karma — including your mother's illness. That is your karma.

**Question:** My karma?

**Feng:** You're in no position to interfere with anyone else's karma. “Interfere” is a big word.

**Question:** It's her life — why is it my karma?

**Feng:** You can't interfere with anyone before you're liberated. There is no separation; she is a picture of yours.

**Question:** She preset her life — her karma — before she came, which led to the illness. That's my karma too?

**Feng:** She is only a picture of yours.

**Question:** Good heavens. So I can master the whole world I see through releasing?

**Feng:** Lester said this many times. Don't be so startled, as though you've only just heard it. *(laughs)*

**Answer:** Ha ha ha ha — this really has refreshed my understanding.

**Question:** So my mother's illness, my relatives' debts and so on are all my karma.

**Feng:** Karma is your desires. Programs are feelings; they are driven by desire.

Taking full responsibility means taking complete responsibility for the whole world you create — including the sun rising, the drift of the dust, the vibration of atoms. You created all of it. You're just not conscious of it. Stay conscious of it, and you can consciously create what you want, rather than what you don't want.

**Question:** Teacher, can I help heal all of them through releasing? Even someone who's been paralysed for years?

**Feng:** I've actually answered this kind of question many times. For questions like “can it do such-and-such?”, I hope you'll all remember something Lester said: *every impossible, no matter how impossible, become immediately a possible ,as you completely released on it.* (Every impossible, no matter how impossible, immediately becomes a possible, once you have completely released on it.)

**Question:** Teacher, what exactly do you mean here by the harmonious working of the universe? For example, if I heal a family member through releasing, is that the harmonious working of the universe?

**Feng:** At the same moment that you have a thought, she will have the corresponding thought, exactly matching. So you don't need to use words or actions to make her change her mind. You only need to deal with your own thinking, and the world will change.

In the end, there's nothing to do but release. *release only*.
