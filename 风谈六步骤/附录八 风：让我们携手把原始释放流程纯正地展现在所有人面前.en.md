## [21 October 2020 — Spread the original Release Method without forcing it on anyone, but don't take money for it]

**Feng:** I spent four years looking for material, releasing at the same time. Later, after going abroad, I came across Lester's later recordings and the 1992 course, and only then did I understand a concise, complete map. It is extremely clear, concise and powerful — a complete map to freedom.

Especially Lester's explanations of the Release Method in his later recordings. Once you've learned the releasing process, focus on the *The Way* series. That is Lester's direct teaching; it will clear up a lot of questions, and you'll also be enormously encouraged.

Spread the original releasing as much as you can, without forcing it on anyone — but don't take a single penny for it.

## [23 October 2020 — Only by keeping it free can the Release Method be kept pure]

**Feng:** Today I guided that group member. Because she had never experienced releasing emotions, I started with the three questions for releasing emotions, and then let her go a step deeper. People who haven't experienced releasing emotions will learn very fast too if they follow the order of the course.

Lester mentioned that if you don't want freedom that much, you'll be pulled back by this world; the only reason you can't succeed is that you don't want it enough.

So even with the method, I still don't think most people can be free. But at least the tool is provided to people, so that those who need help can be helped.

But in the course of releasing I had a strong intuition: any charging will damage the presentation of this original method. So I hope that when you spread this method, you won't use it to take a single penny.

Most importantly, you must first do it yourself before you can teach others. If you can't achieve abundance yourself — can't get everything you want through releasing — you can't teach others either. You'll inevitably change it and add some limiting ideas to it.

If you can't run an organisation just through releasing, but only through charging, you can't keep it pure.

## [30 October 2020 — Feng: I hope the Release Method stays free to spread]

**Online friend:** What do you think of charging for sharing it?

**Feng:** My personal hope is that it can stay free to spread, but that's up to you. Because these things were restored through the selfless dedication of many people; I only did some of the work.

There are two reasons I hope it stays free. One is that there are already enough Release Method organisations today, and none of them is about awakening. I see being free of charge as one way to guarantee its purity. The second reason is that, for you yourself, charging actually hinders your growth. That's my view, but it isn't compulsory; you can still choose to do what you want. *(laughs)*

Finally — keep releasing constantly, and follow the Six Steps all the way through.

**Online friend:** Were the courses Lester taught during his lifetime actually paid or free?

**Feng:** At first they were free. Then hardly anyone came to listen, and those who did listen didn't use it, so later he started charging.
