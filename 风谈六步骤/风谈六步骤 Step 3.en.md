## Until releasing becomes a habit, follow the process — don't just “be aware”

**[24 October 2020] Feng:** As soon as you recognise wanting approval or wanting control, you'll immediately have a natural tendency to let it go, and it will dissolve before you even have time to ask.

**Question:** As soon as I'm aware of it, it goes — sometimes. Other times it's still there.

**Feng:** So use the process. Don't use your own invention, “just being aware” — it won't go. The process is already very simple: allow yourself to become aware of the feeling, become aware of whether it's wanting control or wanting approval, and then let it go.

If you try to skip the process, it won't work. It may work once or twice, but it won't always work, because the momentum of the head is very strong. If you don't use this technique, your habit of suppressing will drag you along.

The releasing process goes against people's habits, so at first you need to use it consciously, until you reach a point where the process carries you along.

**[22 October 2020] Question:** Feng, once you've released to a certain degree, like you, do you still release step by step using the process? Or does any want leave as soon as you're aware of it, so you don't need to ask the questions any more?

**Feng:** *floats up and out* — it surfaces and leaves like a wave. Later on in releasing, the momentum becomes very strong. As long as you make a decision to release, it carries you along; you don't need to work at the operation the way you did before.

## [23 October 2020 — The key to releasing is the process, and releasing constantly. Don't confuse it with awareness and witnessing in other methods]

**Clear Stream:** Feng, if I'm just aware of a tendency to want and release it directly, without being aware of whether it comes from wanting approval or wanting control — would that work? If not, why not?

**Feng:** You can only release when you're clearly aware of it. When it rises to the surface, you'll be aware of it directly, and then you'll have a natural tendency to let it go.

**Yitian:** So the key to releasing is still the ability to be aware, right?

**Feng:** No — it's the process. However good your awareness is, if, at the point in the process where you should be releasing wanting control, you're aware of something else, you'll get stuck.

**Mingkong:** Normally, should I release when feelings and emotions come up, or should I be aware all the time and actively ask about my present feeling and actively go through the process?

**Feng:** Use the process actively.

**Zhaoyan:** Should I release things one by one, person by person?

**Feng:** Not thing by thing — feeling by feeling. Be consciously aware of your feelings and of the wanting control and approval they stir up.

**Yitian:** So the key to releasing is being able to be aware of that “wanting”?

**Feng:** The key to releasing is the process. I can't say it's awareness, because awareness is something very easily confused.

**The Great Way Is Simple:** How is releasing different from watching as an onlooker?

**Feng:** When you're watching as an onlooker, do you want control or want approval?

**The Great Way Is Simple:** You just don't take part — you just watch.

**Feng:** Many people say, “I'm just witnessing.” That means putting yourself in the position of a witness, but it's a pretence, because you're still suppressing feelings. Those drives are still running you from deep down; wanting control and approval are still driving you.

**The Great Way Is Simple:** May I ask what the essential difference between the two is?

**Feng:** One is suppressing, the other is releasing.

**The Great Way Is Simple:** I understand watching as natural non-action; suppressing is still action.

**Feng:** You can't reach non-action this way. Lester said there are two kinds of non-action. One is the lowest state, apathy. The other is the highest state, peace — everything is perfect, everything is infinite, there's nothing to change. If you deliberately try to reach non-action or “go with the flow” now, which kind do you think you'll be?

Lester has a better summary: “The method is simple, and the method is easy. Let the feelings come up, see that they're wanting approval or control, and let them go. Following the six steps of this method will take a few months to empty out all your garbage.”

The key to the Release Method is, of course, constant releasing — not constant witnessing. Notice the feeling you have now, see whether it's wanting control or wanting approval, and release it.

I've practised many types of meditation and different methods that emphasise awareness, and they really are different from the Release Method. Using those experiences to operate the releasing process will only create obstacles.

**Clear Stream:** When I'm aware of wanting control or approval, I only need to be aware of the tendency to want — I don't need to be aware of what the approval or control is about, whatever the content is? Is that right?

**Feng:** Yes. Do you all think there has to be content? There doesn't. That's not how this world was created. First you created a mind; first there was the tendency of the fear of death, and only then wanting control and approval. Then the AGFLAP emotions, then thoughts, then thoughts projected the material world, and the material world linked up into the content of events.

So the content of events is a result. It isn't events that cause the tendency to want; it's wanting that causes events. Wanting drives every thought that creates the world. So if you're aware of the want, that's the cause; you don't need to match it to an event.

**Clear Stream:** So that's it. I had the direction completely wrong before. Releasing the want is really letting go at the level of the cause, isn't it?

**Feng:** Wanting is the most fundamental cause.

## [18 October 2020 — No effort is needed to be aware of feelings]

**Feng:** When the process says “allow yourself to become aware”, it doesn't mean digging forcefully. It means whatever you become aware of while doing nothing at all, right now. There's no need to summon effort or to use control.

Why doesn't the course teach you “how to become aware”? Because “how” contains a mental effort.

For example, ask yourself whether, right now, you want control or want approval. You'll be aware of it straight away. If you aren't, then you want to make yourself be aware. Wanting things to go your way is wanting control. It's very simple.

So, I'll say it again: keep the Six Steps firmly in mind. Keep them close by and pick them up and look at them at any time. Any problem with releasing can be answered by the Six Steps.

## [17 January 2021 — CAP isn't the top. Staying in CAP will develop AGFLAP. Only by fully releasing the three basic wants will you be free]

**Feng:** Lester often talks about releasing AGFLAP, but he doesn't talk about releasing CAP.

**Tiancheng:** Why do you say that?

**Feng:** Because CAP are the so-called positive emotions: Courageousness, Acceptance, Peace. CAP eats up AGFLAP. In courageousness you naturally tend to take good actions; in acceptance you accept the harmony and perfection that were there all along.

But reaching CAP won't remove all AGFLAP. You have to release wanting control and approval to remove all AGFLAP.

That's like when lots of people ask why CAP needs releasing. Because it isn't the top; CAP is relative.

Even if you reach CAP, until you've released the three basic wants, you won't completely clear AGFLAP.

Because CAP are so-called good feelings, and good feelings correspond to bad feelings.

If you want to control a good feeling, you'll develop fear — fear of losing it. If you want more, you'll develop lust — wanting to get more good feelings.

If you don't reach the top, AGFLAP will keep being developed — unless you reach *nirvikalpa samadhi* and make infinite being your permanent identification. Then you won't develop AGFLAP any more.

**Tiancheng:** Can you reach *nirvikalpa samadhi* by releasing enough wanting control and approval?

**Feng:** You also need to release the fear of death.

**Tiancheng:** Do you need to release until you make contact with the deeper sense of separation, to reach *nirvikalpa samadhi*?

**Feng:** Release the sense of separation that you are this body. In the instant you release it, you reach samadhi, because you've identified with infinite being.

When you're in CAP, all you can say is that your current emotional state has reached CAP — you could say your garbage has been emptied to a certain degree.

Release the three basic wants and clear out all AGFLAP.

Then you're free, and once you're free you can be master of the mind.

To clear out all AGFLAP you need to reach imperturbability; CAP alone isn't enough. When you're in CAP, it shows you still have corresponding negative emotions very deep down. But in the CAP state, you're better able to dig down (*dig down*) and release those very deep feelings.

## [11 January 2021 — What to do when you feel no feeling, or the feeling is vague]

> **Translator's note:** The original heading says “2020.1.11”, which appears to be a typo for 2021.

**Feng:** That's why I don't recommend people turn their own experience into a method. Always go back to the original process, the complete process.

First, relax your body. As soon as you notice you're forcing your releasing, stop and relax your body, because many people, from past wrong habits, are used to forcing.

When you release, if you're trying to find a feeling, that's escaping the feeling you have now. You escape your present feeling twenty-four hours a day.

Releasing isn't an activity that takes enormous energy. Some people may have the wrong idea about releasing because of wrong ways of practising before.

Accept unclear feelings. An unclear feeling is a feeling; you need to recognise that. There's never a time when you have no feeling — only times when you don't treat a feeling as a feeling.

Lester specifically mentions this *vague feeling*. A feeling is vague because you're escaping the feeling. Go and feel your vagueness.

No feeling is a vague feeling, or an unobvious feeling. Consciously notice your vagueness. Notice it first; don't be in such a hurry. If, when you go to feel, your feeling is “no feeling”, don't go looking for another feeling. Just allow this “no feeling” to be here. First notice this “no feeling” — or call it a blank feeling — consciously notice it, until you recognise it as a feeling.

No feeling, or a vague feeling, is only you escaping the feeling. It isn't really no feeling.

Your first suppressed feeling is the fear of death (wanting to survive). Then it developed wanting approval and control, which developed the nine categories of emotion, and many, many individual feelings.

In the Release Method, when we say “emotions”, we mean those nine emotions of AGFLAP and CAP.

When we say “feelings”, individual feelings, emotions and wants — all of them are feelings.

## [27 November 2020 — Releasing feelings is not allowing and accepting feelings]

**Feng:** “Allowing feelings to come up and leave” is indeed a simple description of the releasing process. So, in method, many Release Method practitioners easily slide into allowing and accepting.

Without entering the Six Steps, you can't reach freedom.

There is something we call “pure awareness”, or *flow of awareness* — the flow of awareness Lester talked about in the early days. He was describing the final state of being. There are many descriptions of it, but the process of getting there is rigorous.

When people try to introspect, or just sit quietly, it's the same as trying to forcibly separate the senses from the life force. Until a person reaches the stage of *pranayama*, the life force flows outward, towards the senses.

So until you have fully mastered the life force (*prana*) at the physical and mental level, trying to use the head to control your pure awareness won't work. When the life force flows inward, the senses close naturally, and only then can awareness become pure, until you reach samadhi — the two stages of samadhi.

The Six Steps are a complete map; failing in any one step will make you stall. When people think in their minds that they're staying in “awareness” and accepting everything, they've actually stopped releasing. They've dropped Step Four, releasing constantly.

**Zaiyan:** How is the life force made to flow inward in the Release Method?

**Feng:** By releasing wanting control and approval at the level of cause, again and again. Because flowing towards the senses = flowing towards the world, and the world = wanting control and wanting approval.

If you keep releasing, the life force will be fully mastered; it no longer flows outward.

**Zaiyan:** It feels as though, with my attention on my abdomen and my spine upright, releasing goes more smoothly.

**Feng:** Releasing doesn't actually need any fixed posture. Putting your attention on the centre of feeling is right.

**Zaiyan:** You mean the whole area of the chest and abdomen?

**Feng:** It's the heart chakra in yoga.

**Zaiyan:** When you enter samadhi, it must be a very deep state of stillness. Can you get there just by releasing anytime, anywhere in daily life? Or do you have to do a certain length of quiet, concentrated releasing to be able to enter it?

**Feng:** There are two kinds of samadhi: samadhi with seed and samadhi without seed. When you reach samadhi without seed, you can enter it at any time, because, as its precondition, the life force of body and mind has been fully mastered.

**Zaiyan:** Could you explain in detail?

**Feng:** It only takes one decision to enter it. I reached samadhi with seed very early on, when I practised Pa-Auk meditation in secondary school; it suppressed my afflictions.

The *nirvikalpa samadhi* spoken of in yogic science is a stage where you've already turned around, similar to the stream-entry the Buddha spoke of — a change in the direction your life flows. For Lester, it's fully achieving Step One.

From then on, every choice you make will naturally tend towards freedom. Only then do you pass from the stream of ordinary people into the stream of the noble ones, and you'll never fall back again. Freedom is the only direction left to go.

**Zaiyan:** How can Step One be achieved? Is it an act of will, or a result of releasing?

**Feng:** It's the result of following the Six Steps through, again and again. You can do it by following one of the pointers in *The Way* — for example, getting everything you want through releasing. When you do this, you gradually discover that what you're looking for in everything is happiness. And the only source of happiness is the mind becoming quiet, letting infinite being show itself more. So you tend more and more towards freedom.

Then you naturally give up trying other directions; your whole life force gathers into one direction, with only one goal left, and that is freedom. That is Step One fully achieved, and you naturally fall into the remaining five steps.

## [23 October 2020 — Releasing has nothing to do with body sensations; releasing is releasing psychological feelings]

**Beautiful Life:** If I release the feeling of hunger, do I not need to eat?

**Feng:** What you release isn't the feeling of hunger. The feeling of hunger is a bodily sensation; it's already a result.

You need to release its cause: the psychological feelings, the wanting control and approval that drive them, and the “fear of death” that drives the wanting control and approval.

The desire to survive is the fear of death; “the fear of death” is the term Lester often uses.

Hunger is a bodily sensation; it's actually already an expression, a result. When you've released wanting control, you can switch off hunger at any time.

*again*, bodily sensations are a result; they're already an expression.

So I've found that many people release on bodily sensations. That isn't the Release Method. What the Release Method releases is *feeling* — emotions — and the cause that drives emotions: wanting.

When you've released these things at the level of cause, the things at the level of result, like bodily sensations, will change.

If you release on the result — on bodily sensations — you'll have endless difficulty, because you can't change someone's posture by smashing their reflection in the mirror.

If you want to change physical pain, release the wanting to change (control) it.

There's no need to release the physical pain; it's a result. If you release your control over it, it will be corrected immediately.

When you release the fear of death, painful bodily sensations will be brought out, but the focus isn't on releasing the bodily sensations; it's on your emotions and basic wants about them.
