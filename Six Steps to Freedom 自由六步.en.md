# Six Steps to Freedom

*Original: [Six Steps to Freedom 自由六步](Six%20Steps%20to%20Freedom%20自由六步.md) · English translation of the Chinese by Claude (Anthropic, Opus 5.5), November 2026 · [Back to English contents](README.md)*

> **About this document.** The original file gives two versions of the Six Steps, each in Chinese and in English. The English is Lester Levenson's own wording, and the Chinese was translated from it by Feng (the note at the end of the original says “Translation: Feng”). Below, for each step, the English already in the repository is shown first, exactly as it appears, followed by Claude's translation of Feng's Chinese. That way you can see where the Chinese stays close to Lester and where it adds to or changes him.

---

## Version 1: The Six Steps to Freedom (taken from Lester's advanced course)

**Step 1**
- *English in the repository:* You must want freedom more than you want the world(approval and control).
- *Claude's translation of the Chinese:* You must want freedom more than you want the world. (You must want freedom more than you want approval and control; approval and control = the world.)

**Step 2**
- *English in the repository:* Make the decision to go free.
- *Claude's translation of the Chinese:* Make the decision to be free.

**Step 3**
- *English in the repository:* All feelings culminate in two wants: wanting of approval and wanting of control.
- *Claude's translation of the Chinese:* All feelings come from wanting approval and wanting control. They are survival programs. Release them.

**Step 4**
- *English in the repository:* Make release constant.
- *Claude's translation of the Chinese:* Release constantly.

**Step 5**
- *English in the repository:* If you get stuck, let go of the wanting to change the stuckness.
- *Claude's translation of the Chinese:* When you get stuck, release the wanting to change the stuck feeling.

**Step 6**
- *English in the repository:* Each time you release, you are happier and lighter. You have more motivation to go free.
- *Claude's translation of the Chinese:* Each time you release, you are happier, lighter, and freer of limitation. As you go on releasing, you become happier and happier, lighter and lighter, and freer and freer of limitation.

> **Translator's note:** In Step 6 the Chinese differs from the English above. It does not mention “more motivation to go free”. Instead it repeats and extends the idea of becoming happier, lighter and less limited. In Step 3, the Chinese adds “They are survival programs. Release them,” which is not in this English version.

## Version 2: The Six Steps (from the 1992 course)

**Step 1**
- *English in the repository:* You must want imperturbability more than you want approval and control.
- *Claude's translation of the Chinese:* You must want imperturbability more than you want approval and control.

**Step 2**
- *English in the repository:* Decide that you can do the methed and be imperturbable.
- *Claude's translation of the Chinese:* Decide that you can do this method and reach imperturbability.

**Step 3**
- *English in the repository:* See that all your feelings culminate in two wants, the want of approval and the want to control. See that immediately and immediately let go of the want of approval and the want to control.
- *Claude's translation of the Chinese:* See that all emotions come from two wants: wanting approval and wanting control. See it immediately, and immediately release the wanting approval and control.

**Step 4**
- *English in the repository:* Make it constant. Release all your wanting approval and wanting to control when alone or with people.
- *Claude's translation of the Chinese:* Make it constant. Release all your wanting approval and control, whether you are alone or with other people.

**Step 5**
- *English in the repository:* If you are stuck, let go of wanting to control the stuckness.
- *Claude's translation of the Chinese:* If you are stuck, let go of your wanting to control the stuckness.

**Step 6**
- *English in the repository:* Each time you use the Method, you are lighter and happier. If you release continually, you'll be continually lighter and happier.
- *Claude's translation of the Chinese:* Each time you use this method, you will feel lighter and happier. If you keep using it, you will keep feeling lighter and happier.

> **Translator's note:** The Chinese word used for “imperturbability” (波澜不惊) literally means “not a ripple stirs” — a calm that nothing can disturb. It is a close and faithful rendering of Lester's word.
