> **Translator's notes**
> - **Hootless** is Lester's coinage, from “not give a hoot”. The Chinese discussion stresses that it means the mind has naturally lost all interest in something, not carelessness or forced indifference.
> - **AGFLAP** is the Sedona Method's acronym for the main emotional states: Apathy, Grief, Fear, Lust, Anger, Pride.
> - **“Release on both sides”** refers to releasing both the attachment (liking) and the aversion (disliking) about something.
> - **“Mike”** in the video is presumably the Michael mentioned in other talks as someone who could manifest effortlessly but didn't go further.
