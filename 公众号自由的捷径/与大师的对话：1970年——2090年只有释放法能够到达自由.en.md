## Conversation 1

**Question:**
All right. Jesus and the Buddha are all-powerful, and they want to help us go free too. So why don't they just do it for us? All-powerful is all-powerful.

**Feng:**
I've answered this question many times. Of course they could, but they don't choose to.

**Question:**
Why not?

**Feng:**
They don't force help on anyone. When you choose freedom, you're free, quite naturally.

**Question:**
So the only reason we aren't free is a very simple one — we don't want freedom at all??? And that's why the masters won't force us?

**Feng:**
Yes. Lester said many times that as long as you want freedom, nothing can stop you. Achieve Step One, and you automatically fall into the other five.

Reaching freedom can be simply *will it* — and then you're free. Not wanting freedom is the only reason you can't be free.

**Question:**
If I really wanted freedom but didn't have the Release Method or any other right method, could I still reach freedom?

**Feng:**
If you really want freedom, you are already free.

**Question:**
So it's possible to go from an ordinary person to a saint in an instant? Has anyone in history done that?

**Feng:**

**Question:**
When a person really wants freedom, can a master use his all-powerfulness to help him gain freedom?

**Feng:**
Yes — that's very simple.

But if you really want freedom, you don't actually need to ask anyone to help you. You'll achieve Step One directly and enter *nirvikalpa samadhi* directly.

**Question:**
Teacher Feng's coming this time has let me see that I'm not free only because I don't want freedom. I used to think freedom was all I wanted.

**Feng:**
Those who want freedom are already free. Everyone left is someone who doesn't want freedom. It's really very simple. Telling whether a person wants freedom is very simple.

**Question:**
So there's no need to push other people to learn it?

**Feng:**
Mm. No need at all.

**Question:**
Because when they really want freedom, they can do it at any time? And the masters will help them, whatever world they're in?

**Feng:**
Yes. The mind always thinks what it's doing is important — that's very amusing. **The mind thinks it is saving the world.** But in fact everyone is infinite being. No one needs to be saved, and no one can be destroyed.

**Question:**
“Telling whether a person wants freedom is very simple” — Teacher, what is the test you mean?

**Feng:**
Suppose someone says they want freedom, yet they aren't free — then you know it isn't true. That's the test I mean. People who want freedom are already free.

**Question:**
Almost no one on this planet can use the ultimate method, “What am I?”. Is that because almost no one really wants freedom? Or is there another reason?

**Feng:**
That's exactly the reason: the will isn't strong enough. That's why a gradual, scientific method is needed — one where you can confirm your gains bit by bit, confirm the source of your happiness, and so turn yourself around.

But with the enquiry “What am I?”, if you become interested in anything else before you've got the answer, you have to start all over again, and everything before was wasted effort. Some people just fix their gaze on the question and turn it into a meditation practice.

**The Release Method encourages you to get everything you want,** and it also gives you simple, easy, concrete ways to do it. It will get you to the end fastest.

Other methods conflict to some degree with the direction of worldly life and give guidance about behaviour, so they'll take you several lifetimes to learn.

If you want to reach freedom in this lifetime, use the Release Method. If you don't mind reaching freedom over the next few lifetimes, you can use other right methods. They help too; they're just slow.

**Question:**
And the ultimate method can't be stopped for a moment either, until you reach the end?

**Feng:**
Mm. With the ultimate question, as soon as you become interested in something else, all your previous effort is lost. It isn't that just staring at the question works. As soon as your interest isn't on getting the answer to this question, all your previous effort is lost.

## Conversation 2

**Feng:**
Only in *nirvikalpa samadhi* can a method of awakening be discovered.

**Question:**
When you attained *nirvikalpa samadhi*, did you have the ability to discover methods?

**Feng:**
Yes. At that time I saw all 112 methods, without missing any.

**Question:**
Did masters get all these methods from samadhi?

**Feng:**
Yes. There is no method that was invented by the head.

That's why you mustn't change anything. You can't use your head to change a method that was discovered in samadhi. Changing the questions or the process is a master's job; no one who is unawakened can do that work.

**Question:**
Could there ever be more?

**Feng:**
No, there won't be more. The paths won't increase. Masters adjust the specific ways of doing them.

**Question:**
Like Lester adding one — the Release Method.

**Feng:**
**Lester didn't add a new one. The Release Method simply hadn't been revealed before; it was revealed for the first time in history.**

I'm saying this to tell you that methods similar to the Release Method are not the Release Method. There are many methods that look like it.

Some people will tell you that the Four Foundations of Mindfulness are the Release Method. Some will say some ancient method is the Release Method, others that some modern method is. But in fact the Release Method was discovered for the first time.

**Question:**
So the Release Method was prepared specifically for this age?

**Feng:**
Yes. Yogananda chose to leave after Lester had fully awakened, and that announced the end of the age of the guru.

Lester could only help a small number of people, and in this age only very few people will awaken. Everyone who has this method has the chance.

There will be many false masters in this age, but in this age only the Release Method can lead to freedom. So anyone who claims to have gone free by another means is a false master.

**I mean after 1970. For those awakening after 1970 — apart from those who were already awake in a previous life — only the Release Method can do it.**

**And anyone who has already awakened by another means will recommend the Release Method to you, whatever method they used themselves, because a master knows everything.**

**Question:**
Not even the ultimate method Ramana taught?

**Feng:**
In theory, the ultimate question is the fastest. But in practice, no one on this planet can use it at present.

**Question:**
What about the Kriya Yoga that Yogananda taught?

**Feng:**
You won't go free in this lifetime. Kriya Yoga's demands are too high. It belongs to Raja Yoga — it's a comprehensive path.

**The Release Method is the only simple, easy method in this age.**

**Question:**
If we don't use it to the utmost, we really are the stupidest people in the world.

**Feng:**
You have no reason not to use the Release Method. Whether for moving towards awakening or getting a better world, it's the most efficient. Not only efficient, but simple.

Because you'll all go through low points, and when you're in a low point it's very easy to give up. If something tempting-looking comes along at that moment, you'll be drawn to it.

**So what I'm stressing is something I know: in the present age, starting from 1970, for these 120 years, only the Release Method can take you to the end.**

**Giving up the Release Method means giving up freedom in this lifetime, whatever other spiritual method you switch to.**
