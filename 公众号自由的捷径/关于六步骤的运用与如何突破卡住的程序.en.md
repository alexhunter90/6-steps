# On Using the Six Steps, and How to Break Through Stuck Programs (29 December 2020)

*Original: [关于六步骤的运用与如何突破卡住的程序](关于六步骤的运用与如何突破卡住的程序.md) · English translation by Claude (Anthropic, Opus 5.5), November 2026 · [Back to English contents](../README.md)*

*Words said in English in the original are kept as they were said and shown in italics.*

---

The Shortcut to Freedom · 29 December 2020, 09:53

**Tiancheng:** Feng, how did you break through stuck programs?

**Tiancheng:** I've noticed I've been stuck these last few days.

**Tiancheng:** This week I shut myself away in a hotel to do releasing.

**Feng:** When you're stuck, release wanting control.

**Tiancheng:** I used Step 5, but I'm still stuck.

**Feng:** But be aware of one thing.

**Feng:** Your karma will push you to act.

**Feng:** So you'll go and do other things.

**Tiancheng:** Yes — I do a bit and then look at my phone, or go out for a walk.

**Feng:** At those times you'll find that whatever you do, as long as you release, you'll handle it with more ease.

**Feng:** Notice this, and it will encourage your releasing.

**Feng:** That's why Lester didn't advise people to go into retreat.

**Feng:** He had his students do it in action.

**Tiancheng:** What does doing it in action mean?

**Feng:**
> “So there's no obstacle in action; the obstacle is identifying with the limited body and mind, identifying with the ego. If you try not to take any action, you won't succeed, because your karma is now driving you to act. So if you try not to take any action, you won't succeed — whatever your karma is, you'll do it. So trying to do nothing is useless; only releasing is useful. Only releasing will give you every way, and the faster you do it, the faster it comes. So action, rather than being a hindrance, is a real help, because when you're acting you're pushed along by feelings, and feelings bring up more feelings as you act, giving you everything to release. So you must do it in the world; you must do it in action. You can't do it cut off from the world. When you cut yourself off, you're only escaping.”

**Feng:** This is from *the way2*.

**Feng:** If you force yourself into retreat, your mind will tend to escape.

**Feng:** Because it's as if you're saying, “Ready — I'm coming to release you now.”

**Feng:** There's actually no need for that.

**Feng:** You can just release.

**Feng:** Release easily.

**Feng:** Releasing continuously doesn't require avoiding action on the level of action.

**Feng:** As long as there's something you want to achieve, you have a goal.

**Feng:** Then you can keep up the motivation to release.

**Feng:** When you're stuck, use Step 5.

**Tiancheng:** I feel continuously stuck.

**Feng:** Then that's continuous wanting control.

**Feng:** Actually, often getting stuck is a good thing.

**Feng:** It means your control programs are surfacing.

**Tiancheng:** How did you break through this stage?

**Feng:** Every time you release wanting control while you're stuck, you dissolve some of it.

**Tiancheng:** I can clearly feel I have more wanting control.

**Tiancheng:** Emotionally, I'm at anger.

**Feng:** This stage gets much better once you've completely stepped out of AGF.

**Feng:** Releasing in LAP is much smoother.

**Feng:** The state of anger also makes people stuck, but you have the energy to handle it.

**Tiancheng:** I remember you shared in the group in September

**Tiancheng:** that during that time you were stuck the whole time and didn't do a single release.

**Tiancheng:** Did you have a phase like that?

**Feng:** Yes.

**Tiancheng:** How did you break through it in the end?

**Feng:** When you feel defeated in releasing, it's very difficult.

**Tiancheng:** Yes.

**Feng:** You have to release consciously.

**Feng:** Decide to release.

**Feng:** At the same time, notice your wanting control.

**Feng:** Release your resistance and opposition.

**Tiancheng:** Release the resistance and opposition by releasing the wants?

**Feng:** Mm.

**Feng:** This stage is very difficult, I know that.

**Tiancheng:** Yes, it's so hard.

**Feng:** Because you'll resist releasing.

**Tiancheng:** The stage I'm at now is higher than ever before.

**Tiancheng:** But I know there's still a lot of pain.

**Tiancheng:** But I feel stuck, and I feel defeated.

**Feng:** Mm.

**Feng:** First you need to be clear that you want to be freer.

**Feng:** Then you need to keep deciding to release.

**Feng:** Release only what's on the surface.

**Feng:** Once what's on the surface has been released, decide again to release what's on the surface.

**Feng:** Keep going by deciding to release, one time after another.

**Feng:** When you get stuck again and again, you release wanting control again and again.

**Feng:** With each release, you should feel happier and lighter.

**Feng:** This is how the Six Steps are used.

**Feng:** When you reach nirvikalpa samadhi, that is the first of the Six Steps

**Feng:** fully achieved.

**Feng:** Fully achieving the first step makes you fall into the remaining five

**Feng:** spontaneously.

**Feng:** So there's actually not much need to go into that in detail.

**Feng:** Before then, you need to keep checking yourself against the Six Steps,

**Feng:** to make sure you're on the way to reaching the first step.

**Tiancheng:** So until we reach nirvikalpa samadhi, we have to keep working hard and can't relax — is that right?

**Feng:** Yes.

**Feng:** As Yogananda said, you still can't relax until you reach nirvikalpa samadhi, because only once you've reached it is it stable, and you no longer fall back.

**Tiancheng:** Mm. So what I need to do now is keep releasing the stuck programs.

**Feng:** Mm.

**Feng:** But don't try to release it all in one go.

**Feng:** Release what's on the surface.

**Feng:** With one release, you'll feel better.

**Feng:** If you release once more,

**Feng:** you'll feel better still.

**Feng:** Do it patiently like this and you'll soon clear out the programs that keep you stuck.

**Tiancheng:** Mm.

**Tiancheng:** When we get stuck, I always wonder whether I need to go back to the stage of releasing emotions.

**Feng:** Ha ha, don't be so quick to send yourself backwards.

**Feng:** The Six Steps don't send you backwards.

**Feng:** All the answers are in the Six Steps.

**Feng:** If anything departs from the Six Steps, don't believe anyone.

**Feng:** Even Lester appearing in front of you could be fake.

**Feng:** But the Six Steps are perfect steps.

**Feng:** Lester mentioned this:

**Feng:** *Do you know what I'm saying? Even if an angel tells you something, if it's not in accordance with Truth, reject it, because there are so many high appearing beings that look like gods that you can be very easily fooled,-until you know the Truth. Truth is the same throughout from infinity to infinity.*

**Feng:** High beings will disguise themselves as God and as masters to fool humans.

**Feng:** So even if a “Lester” appears in front of you, as long as what he tells you goes against the Six Steps, don't believe him.

**Feng:** Just keep carrying out the Six Steps.

**Tiancheng:** OK.

**Tiancheng:** Thank you.

**Feng:** You're welcome.

> **Translator's notes**
> - **The long indented passage** is Lester's teaching as Feng quoted it in Chinese, from a source he calls *the way2*. The original English wasn't in the repository, so this is translated from the Chinese. The angel passage near the end is Lester's original English, as given in the original.
> - **AGF and LAP** are Sedona shorthand for the lower emotional levels: Apathy, Grief, Fear; and Lust, Anger, Pride. Above them are CAP: Courageousness, Acceptance, Peace.
> - **Step 5** of the Six Steps is “Let go of wanting to control.”
> - **Nirvikalpa samadhi** (the original writes 涅比卡帕三摩地) is the yogic term for the highest, fully stable absorption in the Self, as used by Paramahansa Yogananda.
> - **Tiancheng** is a group member.
