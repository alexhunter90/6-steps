**Question:**

So, to reach freedom you have to become a superman — an all-capable superman. If there's even the slightest thing he can't do, he can't be free. Is that right?

**Feng:**

Mm. Freedom is being your infinite being; it is releasing every limitation. If your mind is completely quiet and your feelings and thoughts are fully released, you are infinite being.

**All-knowing, all-powerful, everywhere present.**

**Question:**

The things mentioned in Yogananda's book *Autobiography of a Yogi* — can an enlightened one do all of them?

**Feng:**

Yes. Everything in *Autobiography of a Yogi*, and everything it doesn't mention.

**All-powerful means all-powerful. There's no compromised interpretation of it.**

**All-knowing, all-powerful and everywhere present means all-knowing, all-powerful and everywhere present, in the literal sense.**

**Question:**

Teacher, Lester said that once you're free you can have a hundred bodies. Have you tried that experiment?

**Feng:**

Ten thousand would be fine too. They're all just a picture. Everything is images. Break down your limiting ideas about freedom.

Although Lester kept saying “all-knowing, all-powerful, everywhere present”, people still understand those words in a limited way.
