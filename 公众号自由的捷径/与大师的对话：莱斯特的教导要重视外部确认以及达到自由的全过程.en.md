# Conversations with the Master | Lester's Teaching: Value Outer Confirmation — and the Whole Process of Reaching Freedom

25 September 2021 · posted by 勇敢的芯2 (“Brave Heart 2”) · read 93 times, shared 4 times · (The Shortcut to Freedom)

**Feng:**

I'll stay for a week, and then disappear into the crowd. A week is already long enough as far as the mind is concerned. (Editor's note: Feng has since left.)

Using the Release Method, you can awaken in a month or two — unless you procrastinate. While I'm here, people's minds will procrastinate, because you'll keep looking outside yourself for answers.

Only the limited, separate self needs an outer master.

Lester said he had two advantages. One was that he knew nothing about the subject; the other was that he had a gun to his head.

My advantage was that I never sought it, and I never met a forerunner who dragged me along.

No one can drag you to freedom. Only you can make that decision.

**Question:**

I've failed many times. Often it isn't that I can't do it — it's that I'm unwilling to.

**Feng:**

*so fearful to be free* — from Lester's time on, he kept saying this to his students. Because almost everyone who uses the Release Method procrastinates unconsciously.

The reason is silly: it's fear of freedom. Fear that being free will mean losing your feelings, losing your body. In fact it won't.

That's why Lester kept stressing: don't be afraid of freedom. When you're free you won't lose anything; you'll only lose your misery.

**Question:**

How did you break through this fear?

**Feng:**

See it, then let it go.

**Question:**

As simple as that!

**Question:**

If I can't release constantly and I still have fear, is it because my wish for freedom isn't strong enough?

**Feng:**

These two video clips can help you.

When you want freedom more than anything else in the world, you'll be free.

What do you want? Get it through releasing. You want a life of material abundance? You want an intimate relationship? **Get everything you want through releasing.**

Then you'll discover that it doesn't bring happiness — that the only source of happiness is your infinite being. And you'll turn around.

*want freedom more than anything else in the world* — if this sentence isn't practical for you,

then Lester has another, more practical one: *get everything you want by releasing only*. What is “everything”? Everything is everything — all your wants.

In your life, every minute of every day that you're dissatisfied, that's wanting. Release the wanting, and you immediately experience beingness.

Something else I want to stress is completely different from what the spiritual scene in the world today tells you: you must **value outer confirmation**.

**Step one: master the world (*mastering the world*). Then master the mind (*mastering the mind*).**

If you feel very good right now but you're powerless over the world, then your subconscious is suppressing those AGFLAP feelings.

So Lester's teaching values outer confirmation. Almost all of his later students forgot this.

**Question:**

What is outer confirmation? I still don't quite understand.

**Feng:**

You think of something and it happens. One effortless thought, and the thing is there — just as in a dream.

**Question:**

That has to be experienced too, doesn't it?

**Feng:**

Of course. Through releasing. Don't compromise.

The first step to freedom is mastering the world. Then you release your craving and aversion towards the world.

But if you can't do it, and you hide “I can't” in your subconscious, you can't be free. You must be a winner.

Lester stressed this to his students again and again, but most of them compromised.

The reason I say you must value outer confirmation is that many people take a superficially good feeling for freedom. Their world hasn't changed, because their subconscious still holds on to those pictures.

A person whose mind has truly changed will inevitably see their world change. Because what is the world? The world is nothing but the projection of a heap of your thoughts.

**Question:**

Teacher Feng, could you describe the whole process of reaching freedom?

**Feng:**

**The whole process of reaching freedom is this: through releasing, you keep becoming lighter and happier. Then you reach the place where you watch everything happen — the place where one effortless thought can create anything. Beyond that, there's nothing else to accumulate in the mind.**

**Question:**

It's only when you set freedom as your goal that you find out how many “can'ts” are hidden inside, needing to be released!

**Feng:**

That's why Lester advocated using goal sheets: their purpose is to pull out the programs hidden in your subconscious.

Don't compromise on your goals. If you can't do something, that's merely a feeling of “can't”. See it, and release it.

Release until you see it achieved right now — taste it, and know it's already yours.

> **Translator's notes**
> - **“Outer confirmation”** (外部确认) means confirming your releasing through visible results in the outer world: your thoughts manifesting, your circumstances changing. Not just through inner feelings.
> - **“A gun to his head”** refers to Lester's heart condition in 1952, when doctors told him he had little time to live. This pushed him to find the answers.
> - **AGFLAP** is the Sedona Method's acronym for Apathy, Grief, Fear, Lust, Anger, Pride.
> - The **“two video clips”** Feng mentions aren't included in the original file.
