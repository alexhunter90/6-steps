The Shortcut to Freedom · posted 5 March 2021, 09:19
Date of talk: 17 November 2020

**Feng:**
Rely on the Six Steps. Start with the Six Steps, finish with the Six Steps. Use your *gain* to confirm your direction.

Constant releasing has become a “task” for you, and that isn't good. You release constantly because every release brings a benefit. That is what Step Six says: each release is lighter and happier. You feel good, so you carry on releasing.

If you just force yourself to release, you'll stop. You won't be able to keep going, because at the same time you are suppressing your desires for the world.

My way is to keep confirming my *gain*, and my direction.

For example, she was just about to listen to music. What does she want to get from the music? Can she get that from releasing? Why not give it a try?

Constantly confirming your gains is what keeps you releasing constantly.

If you say, “I can do it, I must release, I'm not allowed to listen to music”, that won't work, because one of your subconscious directions is listening to music, and you've pushed it down. Your conscious and subconscious minds are pulling against each other. If you don't look at that, it won't succeed.

In fact, you don't need to enjoy anything. *you don't need enjoy anything, be joy!*

Write down the things you think might bring you happiness — where you think happiness is. Not intellectually, but where you actually go looking for happiness.

If you suppress these desires, you can't possibly achieve Step One. You can force yourself to do your homework and your job, but you can't force yourself to release.

Let me go into Lester's favourite teaching in detail: *get everything by releasing only* (getting everything just by releasing).

What do you want? How are you planning to go about it? Why not get it through releasing? That is how you practise it: you choose to release.

Sometimes you might spend a whole afternoon doing other things and not releasing. That's because your subconscious is looking for something. There's no way to force yourself to release.

You need to see what you're looking for, and ask yourself, “Can I get this from releasing?” See which way is lighter, easier and happier, and so choose releasing.

That's why I say experiences of success are important. You have to get a benefit first. Without a benefit, you won't do it — that's your mind. It will do everything it can to avoid releasing and resist releasing. I remember saying before that it was valuing Step Six that truly brought me into constant releasing.

Why do so many people want to sleep as soon as they start releasing? Simple: compared with forced releasing, sleeping is more comfortable. The mind always tends towards whatever is more comfortable.

That's why Lester always had you confirm your *gain*. This is very important. Step Six can't be overlooked, so you have to keep getting benefits.

Starting from each release — every single time you release, you need to feel better. That's the only way you'll keep going. As soon as you notice forcing or effort, relax at once and release the wanting control.

If releasing is more comfortable for you than sleeping, you'll naturally tend towards releasing. It really is that simple.

So I say that because you haven't seen the whole path, you naturally have doubts. Once you've experienced the Six Steps, you'll know how perfect they are.

All of Lester's guidance is about the Six Steps. Without them beside you, you can drift off the path without even knowing it.
