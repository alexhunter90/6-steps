**Foreword:**

This article is put together from things Feng shared at different times. Its aim is to explain to beginners why, in passing on the Release Method today, we stress the word “original”. In Larry's *The Abundance Book* and Hale's *The Sedona Method*, the releasing methods have both changed, to some degree, the simple and efficient original method that Lester created. These changes not only weaken the original method's effect, they also can't take you to ultimate freedom. So, to tell the methods apart, we now generally call the releasing method Lester taught “the original Release Method”. And because Lester settled in Sedona, Arizona, after his awakening, the method is also called “the original Sedona Method”.

Precisely because the original Release Method is so simple, it can seem that adding some other method, adding techniques you've worked out yourself, adding a little of this and changing a little of that, could make up for something, improve on it, make it more effective. In fact, the best way to learn releasing is to do it steadily, exactly as given. An enlightened master could not possibly create a flawed method that still needs perfecting and give it to you to practise.

Keep it simple and do it as given, and you'll discover how simple and efficient it is.

## 01

**Shared on 17 September 2020**

**I Am Infinite:** You know Lester's recordings really well.

**Feng:** Mm. Every one I could find, I've listened to at least twenty times. In *The Way* Lester gives several suggestions. He says that by doing any one of them you can be free. One is *get everything and anything by releasing only* — getting everything just by releasing. He explains further that this means you'll release constantly, because you have things you want every moment.

Another is *release the fear of dying directly*. *The Way* must be part of the advanced course; it was spoken to graduates of the Release Method course, people who were all releasing well. I listened to it while studying abroad. There were a few recordings I particularly loved; they resolved a lot of my confusion, so I listened to them often, and some sentences stuck firmly in my memory — the *The Way* series, for example. Because the early recordings say very little about releasing, I listened to those a few times and then stopped. But the later recordings revolve around the Release Method.

Then I watched the 1992 Release Method course. The way they taught the Release Method then is different from how all the various students teach it now, and it fits better with the path Lester describes in the recordings: first find the feeling of releasing, then release wanting control and wanting approval, and then, when you can see the wanting to survive (the fear of death), release it.

**I Am Infinite:** Did Lester teach it personally?

**Feng:** No — Hale and a woman. But Lester was still alive then. Hale was the *workshop leader*, a Release Method teacher Lester had appointed.

**I Am Infinite:** So while Lester was alive nobody dared to mess with it, and as soon as he'd gone they all started fooling around with it!

**Feng:** Later Hale set up his own organisation, and lots of things got added to the Release Method. It became very complicated, when at first it was taught very simply. *The Abundance Book* also has a workbook. I looked at that book: it basically matches Lester's process, but adds a lot of its own material. Later I summed up how the different books teach it. Larry's Abundance Course follows Lester fairly closely in its process, but its actual releasing method doesn't match. Hale's book uses questions in its releasing method, which fits better, but its process doesn't match. Both of them made the method complicated.
