## ► How to move towards freedom faster

### (1) Use releasing to the utmost

**20 November 2020**

**Question:** I'd like to ask your advice. How can I move towards freedom faster?

**Feng:** Use it constantly. Use it to the utmost. I can't give better advice than that. I strongly recommend you use it on everything. Make use of those goals; use constant gaining to spur yourself to release and to boost your momentum in releasing. If you get into the habit of using it on every one of your goals, you'll soon get to that place.

---

### (2) Value goals

**2 January 2021**

**Feng:** Value goals. In theory, you can take freedom directly as your goal. But I know that most people who say they want freedom are suppressing their longing for the world. Why did Lester like the fourth pointer (getting everything just by releasing) best? Because it means the most to people; it's the most practical.

**S:** You took freedom directly as your goal, didn't you?

**Feng:** I did, but I also used that teaching. I wrote down all the things I wanted.

**S:** I combine them too.

**Feng:** The first few pointers often lose their practicality and become mere theory.

**R:** I can't do those yet *(laughs)*. I'd better get everything I want first.

**S:** Becoming a success is still necessary.

**R:** I'm using the first few pointers too — getting all happiness from within, facing the fear of death directly. I'm using them all.

*(Feng posts the four pointers Lester gave in* The Way *part 2.)*

> **Lester:** So what do you need to do? In the previous four days of the course I summed it up.
>
> The first lesson is that you must want freedom more than you want the world. Following from that, you should get all your happiness from within yourself — get it just through releasing. That's lesson one.
>
> Lesson two is: you must make the decision to be free, and then you do it. Actually, if you decide to be free, you will do it. It's that simple. If you're not free, then you haven't made the decision to be free. Because once you make the decision, everything you do afterwards will be towards freedom. The most basic thing is releasing constantly.
>
> The third lesson is to face the fear of dying directly and release it. Release the fear of dying directly. As you know, underneath approval and control is the fear of dying. So get rid of this driver at the very bottom, and you're finished.
>
> The fourth is: from now on, get every single thing you want through releasing. I'd guess this is the most practical of the four.
>
> How successful are you now? Are you still doing things through the eyes of the little ego most of the time? Remember, when we do things through releasing, it's effortless. If you're putting in effort, that's driven by the ego.

**Feng:** Practicality — this is very important. Why did Lester say the fourth is the most practical? Because you often can't get the earlier ones working.

**R:** I used the first one for a while. It didn't push me along as well as the fourth, but the first one has already made me grow incredibly fast. *(laughs)*

### (3) Use the things you want to spur yourself to release

**30 December 2020**

**Feng:** You need to set a goal you really want. You need motivation.

**Question:** Is a million what I really want?

**Feng:** Do you feel that excitement?

**Question:** But once I'm free, I'll have everything — so why don't I go straight for freedom?

**Feng:** That's fine too. Taking freedom directly as your goal is fine too.

**Question:** Do you have any suggestions?

**Feng:** You can write down everything you want.

---

### (4) What matters is whether you *can* do it

**13 December 2020**

**Feng:** Say your goal is a million, and now you say you don't want money any more. Great — then once you've manifested the million, throw it in the river. *(laughs)* What matters is whether you can, not the goal itself. Releasing on goals brings out the opposing programs and puts you in the position of master of mind and matter, at the point where you can achieve a goal perfectly and effortlessly. *By the way*, if you really don't want that million, just throw it away once you've got it.

---

### (5) Is your “not wanting” just “not daring to dream”?

**2 January 2021**

**Feng:** Dare to dream.

**Question:** Could I become a billionaire through releasing too?

**Feng:** Of course. A couple of days ago, someone in the group also felt he didn't want any goals — until I suggested one to him: go to the Eastman School of Music, the best in the world, find a blonde girl and live the romantic life he loves most. He lit up instantly. So dare to dream. The reason many people feel they don't want the world is simply that they don't dare to dream — they've suppressed it. They think it's impossible, so they don't want the world. That isn't really wanting freedom.

**Question:** I've found that I was suppressing it. I thought it was impossible and didn't dare to dream.

**Feng:** *dare think big* — Lester said that. Dare to think big.

**Question:** But so far I haven't achieved even one small goal. In that situation, can I go straight to thinking big?

**Feng:** If it's not a goal you really want, you'll have no motivation. One purpose of a goal is to give you motivation to release. Once that group member found a goal that excited him, his releasing sped up.

---

### (6) Let goals give you the motivation to release constantly

**8 January 2021**

**Feng:** You can set any goal you want, as long as it gives you motivation to keep releasing.

**Question:** So I don't have to practise on small goals first to build up momentum? As long as the goal helps me release constantly, it's meaningful for me — have I understood that right?

**Feng:** Mm. If that's what you want most, then use that.

**Question:** I understand. Thank you.

---

### (7) Once you've completely released, every impossible becomes possible

**22 December 2020**

**Question:** Teacher, can releasing heal a family member's illness? Or achieve other things — to do with money, career, relationships, or anything else at all?

**Feng:** For questions like “can it do such-and-such?”, I hope you'll all remember something Lester said: “Every impossible, no matter how impossible, immediately becomes a possible, once you have completely released on it.” (*every impossible, no matter how impossible, become immediately a possible ,as you completely released on it.*)
