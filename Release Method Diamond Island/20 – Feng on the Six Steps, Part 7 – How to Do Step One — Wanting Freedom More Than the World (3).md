## ► How to do and achieve Step One, “wanting freedom more than the world” (3)

**28 November 2020**

**Tiancheng:** Feng, are you there? I'd like to hear you talk about the actual experience of *nirvikalpa samadhi*. (Note: fully achieving Step One of the Six Steps corresponds to “stream-entry” in the Āgama sutras and *nirvikalpa samadhi* in yogic science. Two of Feng's explanations of *nirvikalpa samadhi* are inserted here first.)

**[11 December 2020] Feng:** *Nirvikalpa samadhi* is actually the full achievement of Step One of the Six Steps. Why? Because you only fully achieve it at the moment you make contact with the infinite.

Making contact with the infinite again and again through releasing, you suddenly find that those seeds have no effect any more; all your motives for looking for happiness elsewhere are gone. Because you have discovered, at the level of experience, the only source of happiness, the seeds no longer take effect.

If you don't make contact with infinite consciousness, you'll always feel there is happiness in the world. And as long as you feel there's happiness in the world, your direction may turn towards the world again. It's that simple.

**[19 November 2020] Feng:** No longer tempted by ego-consciousness, tending completely towards freedom — this is Step One of the Six Steps. In yoga they call it *nirvikalpa samadhi*: the start of the real work. If you haven't reached this point, you may fall back. If you have reached it, you won't fall back. Truly reaching Step One, you won't sink back down. Entering the stream (stream-entry): no more going backwards.

Insight into the source of happiness will bring you to Step One. After that, every decision you make is inward — each one is undoing your bonds and moving towards your real being.

**Feng:** I only learned that term (*nirvikalpa samadhi*) later. I studied Southern-transmission and Northern-transmission Buddhism for many years, and learned Pali specifically to read the Pali Canon. So at the time I immediately went back to the Āgama sutras, and I knew deeply what the Āgamas mean by “stream-entry” — stream-entry, entering the stream. When you fully achieve Step One of the Six Steps, every inclination and choice leads towards freedom. The knowledge that sees suffering is the forerunner of achieving Step One. You see that the direction of the world = suffering, and that the direction of freedom — infinite being — is the only source of happiness. Then you spontaneously stop all your attempts to find happiness in the world by bashing your head against it until it bleeds.

**Tiancheng:** How can you quickly reach Step One in actual experience — that is, the stage of stream-entry?

**Feng:** Follow the Six Steps all the way through. Of the Six Steps, the one people most easily overlook is Step Six, but Step Six is exactly the key to achieving Step One. Because, in your actual experience, you are looking for happiness in the direction of the world. So you need to put into practice Lester's favourite teaching: *get everything by releasing only*. Every time you want something, get it through releasing; that gain will make you want to release even more. Keep confirming your *gain*, and it will keep you releasing constantly — until you find that your goals are being achieved one after another, and you still aren't happy. Then you will “discover”, in your actual experience, that nothing at all can bring you happiness. Instantly, you will become aware of the one and only source of happiness. At that moment, the direction of flow changes immediately — a complete change in the direction your life flows. You have turned around completely. That is achieving Step One fully: wanting freedom more than wanting the world. After that, just as Lester said, you keep falling into the remaining five steps.

**23 October 2020**

**Mini:** Will children and family hold me back from going free?

**Feng:** No. Lester said that the only thing stopping you from being free is that you don't want it. That's the only obstacle.

---

**26 November 2020**

**Feng:** A person who has reached *nirvikalpa samadhi* is like a cosmic Google with infinite knowledge — a cosmic search engine.

**Tiancheng:** Have they reached all-knowingness?

**Feng:** Not all-knowing the way a master is, but they can access all-knowingness at will, and decide at any time to draw knowledge out of samadhi.

---

**23 December 2020**

**Z:** If you turn from the world to within, do you naturally become a super-Google?

**Feng:** Yes. You know that the only source of happiness is infinite being, and other directions no longer have any pull, so all your decisions go in its direction. That brings you immediately to *nirvikalpa samadhi* (infinite being, in which the seeds no longer take effect). In infinite being you can know everything — that's what the cosmic Google comparison means. When you've released to the point where “the only source of happiness is infinite being” has become your complete experience, you naturally stop looking for happiness anywhere in the world. At that point your direction of flow has changed, and you automatically enter *nirvikalpa samadhi*. Once you've entered it, you won't come out again or go backwards, because the seeds of retreat no longer have any power.

**Z:** I feel I want and want to do less and less. Things that used to seem very serious, I don't feel like doing any more. I'm heading in the direction you describe.

**Feng:** Follow the Six Steps all the way through. That way you can get all happiness from within. Or turn yourself around by bringing goals about through releasing — *get everything by releasing only* — and that will keep you on the Six Steps too.

---

**27 November 2020**

**Zaiyan:** How can Step One be achieved? Is it an act of will, or a result of releasing?

**Feng:** It's the result of following the Six Steps through, again and again. You can do it by following one of the pointers in *The Way* — for example, getting everything you want through releasing. When you do this, you gradually discover that what you're looking for in everything is happiness. And the only source of happiness is the mind becoming quiet, letting infinite being show itself more. So you tend more and more towards freedom. Then you naturally give up trying other directions; your whole life force gathers into one direction, with only one goal left, and that is freedom. That is Step One fully achieved, and you naturally fall into the remaining five steps.
