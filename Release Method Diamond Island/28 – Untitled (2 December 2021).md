2 December 2021 · The Shortcut to Freedom · read 14 times, shared 2 times

**Feng:** While you're releasing, the mind is what gets in your way.

> **[Screenshot 1, translated — group chat, 22 February 2021, Feng]**
>
> **Feng:** While you're releasing, the mind is what gets in your way:
> **Feng:** its content, the things you've presupposed —
> **Feng:** even the idea of how long you think it will take to release something —
> **Feng:** this idea itself is getting in your way.
> **Feng:** Just do this one release well.
> **Feng:** There's no need to presuppose that this time you'll release all of it,
> **Feng:** or that it will take so many times to release all of it.
> **Feng:** Just release, and see what happens.
> **Feng:** Don't rush. Enjoy the process of each release.
> **Feng:** Keep using the releasing process.
> **Feng:** The things you think are hard to release — that's the mind telling you they're hard.
> **Feng:** Ignore the mind and go straight to the process.
> **Feng:** The things you think are easy or trivial and so overlook — that's the mind telling you they're trivial.
> **Feng:** Ignore the mind there too, and go straight to the process.
> **Feng:** Sometimes you see a feeling and think it isn't the main one.
> **Feng:** You badly want to release “the main one”,
> **Feng:** and get it over with quickly,
> **Feng:** so you suppress the feeling that's coming up right now,
> **Feng:** and this is very important.
> **Feng:** The mind's judgements that this one or that one is more important have no basis.
> **Feng:** Whatever is surfacing is what matters.
> **Feng:** If you suppress it, you're living in the mind.
> **Feng:** You're using the mind to presuppose what you need to release,
> **Feng:** including how many times and how long.
> **Feng:** When you're truly releasing, there's no need for that.
> **Feng:** Keep open the possibility of releasing all of it in one go.

> **[Screenshot 2, translated — 22 February 2021, Rayna and Feng]**
>
> **Rayna:** What about body sensations? Will they go away later? Will it be any better by the time of my CPA exam? Or will they follow me for the rest of my life if I don't go free? 😓
> **Feng:** What do you feel about the body sensations?
> **Feng:** Do you want to control them?
> **Feng:** Want to change them?
> **Feng:** Just release that.
> **Feng:** If the body sensations are bothering you, release the being bothered.
> **Feng:** Use releasing to solve problems.
> **Feng:** A few releases, and the whole of a long-standing habit is released — can you imagine that?
> **Feng:** But very often that's how it is:
> **Feng:** a few minutes, not a long, bogged-down struggle.
> **Feng:** In the videos you can see it's a few minutes.
> **Feng:** If it's taking too long, you're bogged down, you're suppressing, you're not releasing.
