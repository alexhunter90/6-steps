Let me explain simply. Why do you pursue happiness in this world? Because, at the level of experience, you mistakenly believe there is happiness in the world. With right view leading the way, all eight parts of the path appear before you. The Buddha called this the “transcendent Noble Eightfold Path”, and your direction begins to turn around. In the Six Steps, this is reaching Step One. So at first the Six Steps are an operating manual; once you truly and fully match Step One, they carry you along. If you truly achieve Step One, every choice and action you make will naturally tend towards freedom.

> “Noble disciple! Suffering, and reflection on suffering; arising…; cessation…; the path, and reflection on the path. Untainted reflection in accord with the Dharma — choosing, discerning, investigating, knowing, keen wisdom, awakening, observing — this is called right view. It is noble, beyond the world, untainted, free of grasping; it rightly ends suffering and turns towards the end of suffering.”

At this point, your every choosing, discerning, investigating, knowing, keen wisdom, awakening and observing naturally turns around. Your whole body and mind turn towards liberation, in accord with untainted reflection. “Untainted” is an old translation term meaning perfect, without flaw. You no longer look for happiness in the imperfect world, so with your whole body and mind you naturally turn around in every choice. So reaching Step One of the Six Steps is actually what Buddhism calls a noble one of the first fruit — the fruit of stream-entry, of entering the stream, liberation through wisdom. You have entered the stream of the noble ones.

You naturally cut off three fetters:

The first is the fetter of doubt: because the whole path appears before you, you cut off all doubt.

The second is the fetter of identity view: because you have seen the flaws of the world, you no longer look for an “I” anywhere, and you no longer take the body to be “I”.

The third is the fetter of clinging to rites and rules: because you see the source of everything, you no longer have the idea that rituals or outside powers are effective, or that they can lead you to the end.

At the same time, the karma that could have taken you into the lower realms is completely cleared. This is the result of completing Step One; the remaining five steps then naturally fall into your experience. Besides in *The Way*, Lester said the same thing in other recordings: “Once you achieve Step One, every action you take will be towards freedom.”
