## Editor's Preface

**Release by Following the Six Steps All the Way**

I began exploring spirituality in the autumn of 2019, and in March 2020 I came across the Release Method. The first time I tried it, I released my feelings about a former colleague and, to my great surprise, suddenly burst out laughing. The descriptions of the Release Method's power in *The Abundance Book*, the story of Lester's awakening, the documentary of people remembering Lester — all of it drew me to want to understand this amazing “Release Method”.

At the time I didn't know about the original Release Method. Later, when I wanted to study releasing more deeply, both Larry's and Hale's books seemed roundabout to me, without a clear focus. So I gave up releasing and went back to the Zero Limits cleaning method. But although I'd stopped releasing for the time being, the Release Method stayed on my mind, and I knew it was the more complete method.

Then Feng appeared. Just like his name, “Wind”, he was like a warm spring breeze blowing away the confusion and stuffiness in the releasing scene. He let everyone know that the “original Release Method” existed, bringing clear guidance and bright hope to those of us groping our way along the path of releasing.

He not only made the key points of practice clear and cleared up all kinds of confusion I had about releasing, he also showed me the core thing you most need to hold on to in order to reach the end: following the Six Steps all the way through.

Whenever people online asked questions about releasing, Feng always answered very patiently and in detail. With his powerful memory and his familiarity with Lester's teachings, he always based his answers on, and quoted from, what Lester had explained. Although he had ranged widely in spiritual matters, he rarely quoted other methods or theories to show himself off unless it was necessary. He made a huge contribution to spreading the original, pure Release Method.

His gentleness and humility, his learning and wisdom, his unremitting pursuit of freedom — every one of these moved me. The real examples of him living “getting everything just by releasing” encouraged me greatly when I was slacking. And finally, when he reached the end through constant releasing, I felt for the first time that “enlightenment” was so close to me.

Feng, who practised the Release Method himself and finally reached freedom, was — after Lester — the window that once again turned my attention towards infinite being. You could say it was Feng's sharing that pulled me back to the Release Method. Without him leading the translation of the original Release Method's English materials, clarifying the unmodified original Release Method and patiently stressing the key points again and again, I believe it would have been very hard for me and everyone else to get onto the right path of releasing.

I must also thank “I Am Infinite”, who runs the WeChat account “Original Sedona Release Method”. It was your rigorous, selfless sharing that let more people access detailed original Release Method material. Thank you so much for all you've given. It was your rigour that created an atmosphere better suited to learning releasing correctly, which led Feng to begin sharing, and finally gave the original Release Method the chance to be revealed to people online who were interested in releasing.

> *(Account card:)* **Original Sedona Release Method** — “We are infinite beings, yet we believed the limiting beliefs in the mind.” — Lester Levenson · 52 original articles

Special thanks also to Tiancheng, who selflessly shared Feng's chat records with everyone. He is very good at asking questions. Without him as an excellent “questioner”, we might never have known many of the key points and reminders about releasing. On the path of exploring releasing, I'm very grateful for the help, reminders and encouragement he gave me, and for his WeChat account “The Shortcut to Freedom”.

> *(Account card:)* **The Shortcut to Freedom** — Lester: “Follow the Six Steps all the way through, and within one or two months you can travel the whole path and reach the end.”

Feng's intensive explanations of the original Release Method over the half-year from September 2020 to March 2021 are undoubtedly excellent material for learning releasing. Feng went over Lester's teachings again, and only then did I understand better what some of Lester's words meant, and discover what I had overlooked before. At first I organised the material with a self-help attitude. Later I took the Six Steps as the main thread, selected some of the content, and strung together the sharings scattered everywhere like pearls, gathering them into a book. That way it's easier for me and others to get an overall understanding of releasing, and it also helps people learning the Release Method to help themselves better, practising without depending on others. That is why I put together *Feng on the Six Steps*.

Because Feng's sharing about the Release Method is built on Lester's explanations, and the two complement each other, I later also put together Lester's explanations of releasing into a book, *Lester on Releasing*. That is how the two releasing books came into being.

To protect privacy, some people's names have been left out of this book or replaced with English letters. Also, because a book shouldn't be too thick and space is limited, some of the original chat content has inevitably been condensed and adjusted. Turning scattered, fragmentary chat records into a book, there are bound to be some errors, omissions and oversights, and I ask for your understanding.

In 1974, Lester organised a training for Release Method instructors, hoping to organise the releasing method into a systematic course anyone could use. Out of inspiration, he made a simple summary of the whole course, and so the Six Steps were born. These six steps to freedom are both the core of the Release Method and the “super satnav” that brings us back on course when our releasing goes astray, letting us release constantly all the way to freedom. When we find releasing full of difficulties, we must have strayed from the Six Steps — we're not on them. All we need to do is come back.

I hope this book, *Feng on the Six Steps*, can guide you in learning to use the Six Steps, help you to help yourself better, and follow the Six Steps through to the end. But anything in the nature of reference material has to be put down in the end. There's no need to accumulate content in the head and develop the mind. The mind is an obstacle; learning needs, above all, personal experience.

As Feng said: “Releasing is a person's most natural ability, but enough mental content will obstruct this ability. When you forget to release, it isn't because you haven't learned enough, or accumulated enough knowledge — it's because you've accumulated too much. When you accumulate a lot, you're developing your mind, and that covers up and weakens your ability to release.”

So this book too has to be put down in the end. Just go back to the Six Steps and keep it simple as you release.

Feng also reminded us that the clarity you get from reading material can't compare with the clarity you get from releasing. Don't become, as Feng put it, someone who fills the head with concepts instead of actually releasing to freedom. So, once more, a reminder to everyone: don't use chatting and reading to put off actual releasing. At first, reading Feng's chat records and joining releasing groups to talk things over with others may help us get started. But however much we read, think and talk, in the end it has to come down to real releasing. The secret of getting ahead is to start doing it as soon as possible.

Lester once said that the Release Method is the only efficient method in the world today for reaching ultimate freedom. In getting to know other spiritual methods and practising releasing, I have truly felt this for myself. When I release constantly, I experience how the Six Steps link together, one into the next. They really are a clear map that guides you along the right path of releasing.

The only way to express love and gratitude to Lester and Feng is to put into practice, all the way, what they taught — to go in the direction they always pointed, to the end, and gain freedom.

If they were here now, I think they might say: “This is the path. This is how I walked it. Do the same, walk the same way, and you will reach the final goal.”

Welcome to the journey of releasing. Value the Six Steps, keep releasing, and release constantly all the way to ultimate freedom. I wish you success.

Let me end with Lester's words: “The path we are on begins here and goes all the way to complete freedom. That's the first point I want to stress. The second is that we can reach it in this lifetime! For many people with the same goal, it usually takes many lifetimes. But we can do it in this lifetime.”

Blessings to all of you on the path. Keep going!

Yiman, 18 November 2021
