**Using Step One of the Six Steps to remind yourself to release**

When we want freedom more than we want control and approval, we're willing to release all the feelings that limit us. When we want control and approval more than freedom, we stop releasing. So Step One is very important. It isn't just a sentence; it's a reminder for when we don't want to release.

*(The original shows a screenshot of the chat below; the text of the chat follows it.)*

![Chat screenshot (Chinese) — 7 November 2020](pictures/2022-04-28-12-53-53.png)

**Feng:** Right now, would you rather stay stuck in this wanting control, or would you rather release it and be free of it?

**D:** Of course I want freedom.

**Feng:** So you want freedom more than controlling these feelings?

**D:** Yes.

**Feng:** Could you let go of wanting to control it?

**D:** I'm stuck.

**Feng:** Do you want to keep controlling this stuckness and stay tangled up with it to the end, or do you want freedom? *(smiles)*

**D:** I want freedom. Released.

**Feng:** Can you see that wanting control now? Has it gone?

**D:** It's gone.

**Feng:** What this shows is how to use Step One — Step One of the Six Steps. If you get stuck, use Step Five. If you don't want to release, use Step One.
