# Feng Shares: On Resisting, Being Unwilling, and Avoiding Releasing

*Original: [风的分享：关于抗拒、不愿意、逃避释放](风的分享：关于抗拒、不愿意、逃避释放.md) · English translation by Claude (Anthropic, Opus 5.5), November 2026 · [Back to English contents](../README-EN.md)*

*Words said in English in the original are kept as they were said and shown in italics.*

> **Translator's note:** All the Lester quotations on this page are translated from the Chinese, mostly from the Chinese edition of his book (*The Way to Ultimate Freedom*). His original English isn't in the repository.

From the series *Feng's Collected Talks* · Release Method Diamond Island · 23 January 2022, 22:59

**Lester:** “You are infinite being right now. You always have been and you always will be; you have no choice about that. Your only choice is to identify with being infinite being, or to identify with being a limited ego.”

**Feng:** “Doing releasing is choosing to identify with your infinite being.”

## 01

**Clear Stream:** In my heart I've always been very clear that I want freedom more than I want the world. That's what has driven my learning and growth all along: wanting liberation and freedom. But when it comes to actually releasing, I sometimes still resist, avoid it or stall, and sometimes I forget to release. Does that show that I only want it in my head, and that inside I don't truly want liberation and freedom more than the world?

What I mean is: just being clear in my mind that I want freedom more than the world isn't enough. It's using Step One of the Six Steps often, to remind myself to release, that's the real motivation for releasing. Right?

**Feng:** You have to experience it. The best way is to get everything through releasing. Otherwise you'll always keep open the possibility of getting happiness from other things in the world. If you can't experience that the source of all happiness is within, you can't fully achieve Step One. Shouting slogans is no use.

➤ Related reading: *How to achieve Step One of the Six Steps, “wanting freedom more than the world”?*

---

## 02

**Shuxin:** Feng, sometimes I'm very listless and don't want to release any more. Is that wanting control I need to release?

**Feng:** Yes — you're resisting doing releasing. Why resist releasing? It's just a feeling of resistance. The feeling of aversion is rather subtle; release it actively.

**Shuxin:** Teacher, could you take me through a step? How do I release this resistance? I'm a bit blocked here and can't move.

**Feng:** First, can you feel the resistance?

**Shuxin:** Really strong resistance.

**Feng:** Next, can you feel the wanting to change it? If you don't know the releasing process, spend four days learning it. If you do know it, you can use it. With the *sedona method* there's no such thing as having a good foundation or a poor one. You don't want to face it, you want to run away from it — notice that want. It's the one most on the surface, and you can release it.

---

## 03

**T:** Teacher, does this mean that if I gain ultimate freedom I can't stay in the world?

**Feng:** It's up to you. Freedom is freedom; there's no compulsion of any kind.

**T:** Can I still stay with my family?

**Feng:** Of course.

**T:** I see that I'm afraid of freedom — afraid that once I'm free I'll lose my family.

**Feng:** There are quite a few masters who are householders. A master can even be a king, like William the Conqueror. Krishna demonstrated the householder master.

**T:** You mean masters who, after going free, still live with their families?

**Feng:** You won't lose your family. You won't lose your beloved world.

**T:** In this age, masters have to live in seclusion.

**Feng:** Living in seclusion means not being known as a master. It doesn't mean you have to hide away in the mountains and forests. Living in the world like an ordinary person is also a form of seclusion.

## 04

> **Translator's note:** This section repeats the start of [Conversations with the Master | Lester's Teaching: Value Outer Confirmation](../公众号自由的捷径/与大师的对话：莱斯特的教导要重视外部确认以及达到自由的全过程.en.md), and the translation matches that one.

**Feng:** I'll stay for a week, and then disappear into the crowd. A week is already long enough as far as the mind is concerned. Using the Release Method, you can awaken in a month or two — unless you procrastinate. While I'm here, people's minds will procrastinate, because you'll keep looking outside yourself for answers. Only the limited, separate self needs an outer master.

Lester said he had two advantages. One was that he knew nothing about the subject; the other was that he had a gun to his head. My advantage was that I never sought it, and I never met a forerunner who dragged me along. No one can drag you to freedom. Only you can make that decision.

**Question:** I've failed many times. Often it isn't that I can't do it — it's that I'm unwilling to.

**Feng:** *so fearful to be free* — from Lester's time on, he kept saying this to his students. Because almost everyone who uses the Release Method procrastinates unconsciously. The reason is silly: it's fear of freedom. Fear that being free will mean losing your feelings, losing your body. In fact it won't. That's why Lester kept stressing: don't be afraid of freedom. When you're free you won't lose anything; you'll only lose your misery.

**Question:** How did you break through this fear?

**Feng:** See it, then let it go.

**Question:** As simple as that!

---

**Lester:** When you have released enough of the “ego”, you naturally feel the peace and joy of the “Self”. This really is a path that gradually reveals the natural state of your infinite nature. You let go of limitation, you let go of misery, but you never lose anything of value, anything good.

On this path of yours, you keep getting out of trouble. On this path you never lose anything; you only have more and more of what you really want, until you have it all. Apart from the bandages and wounds we impose on ourselves, we find that on the spiritual path we never lose anything.

— *The Way to Ultimate Freedom*, Chapter 3: Spiritual Growth

---

You won't turn into a zombie, and you won't disappear or go off with a light. You simply let the body go through what you preset for it before, and then, when its time comes, you leave the body joyfully, throwing off this worn-out coat. You never die. The people around you may say you die, but for you, you don't. You consciously let go of the body, like throwing off a worn-out coat. But you only let go of it once the body has finished the journey you preset for it. I'm telling you this now so that when you're enlightened you won't be afraid of dying.

So reaching the ultimate state isn't disappearing into nothingness. It's getting free of the limitations of the body and entering your all-presence. You must have a strong desire for this. The only obstacle stopping you from returning to infinite being is the desire to be a limited body. When this desire turns into wanting to be free of the body's limitations, you are on the path of realisation. But to go the whole way, your desire for complete freedom must be greater than your desire to be a limited body.

The reason so few people go the whole way is that, for most of us, the subconscious desire to be a body is greater than the conscious desire for complete freedom. If you can't face this squarely and see what your real desire is, you can't gain complete freedom, complete enlightenment. The only reason you are limited by a physical body is that, at the subconscious level, you strongly desire to be this limited body. When, at the subconscious level, your desire for complete freedom is greater than your conscious desire to be a limited body, you can gain freedom very quickly — and that is where ultimate happiness is.

The change from before realisation to after is only a change of viewpoint. Before enlightenment you think you are a limited body, and all bodies and actions seem so real to you. After enlightenment, you see it as a dream, a film.

— *The Way to Ultimate Freedom*, Chapter 32: Enlightenment — Letting Go of the Unconscious
