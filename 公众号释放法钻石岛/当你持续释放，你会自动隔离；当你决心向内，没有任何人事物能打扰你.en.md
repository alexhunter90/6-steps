**▲ Topic: When you release constantly, you withdraw automatically; when you resolve to go within, nothing and no one can disturb you**

**Shared on 23 October 2020**

**Feng:** If you go about it by escaping — pushing those feelings deep down — they become your karma, and they'll drive you out of your withdrawal by force.

When you release constantly, you withdraw automatically.

The Six Steps don't tell you to quit your job, and they don't tell you to isolate yourself. They only tell you to release constantly. When you release constantly, withdrawal happens automatically.

Constant releasing doesn't depend on a quiet environment, because your world is only the projection of your thoughts; you can't change the cause by doing something to the effect. If you feel it's noisy, that's just a feeling. Go back to Step Three and release the wanting control and wanting approval in the feeling you have now.

At first you'll find it difficult, so start by using all the time when you're not doing mental work. When you do this, constant releasing builds momentum, which makes your releasing happen more and more easily. Then you'll be able to release while you're doing things too.

Keep releasing, and you'll find you don't need to do things any more, because you're no longer creating that picture. You're no longer creating the picture of “I need to work”.

**Shared on 7 January 2021**

**Y:** “When you release constantly, you withdraw automatically” — does that mean that once you're in constant releasing, naturally nobody disturbs you?

**Feng:** Of course. The outside world is your projection to begin with. If you're disturbed, it can only mean you want to be disturbed. You created that situation of being disturbed with your thoughts.

**Y:** When you were in retreat, didn't you have any disturbances?

**Feng:** No. Once you resolve to go within, nothing can disturb you.

**Y:** Is it that there are stimuli outside but you don't react? Or that outside there really, naturally, is no disturbance at all?

**Feng:** It's the same thing. Outside there are only your thoughts. At first you're still creating some events, but that's your desires disturbing you.

Later in the releasing, especially when you're concentrating on releasing the fear of death and the wanting control and approval have mostly gone, the world doesn't disturb you at all. In a forest this would be even more obvious: venomous snakes and wild beasts would keep well away from you.

**Y:** For example, you're in your retreat room and someone knocks on the door, but it doesn't affect you at all? Or is it that once you're in constant releasing, nobody comes knocking at all?

**Feng:** Once you've released enough wanting control and approval, nobody knocks at all.

**Y:** I see.
