## 01

### ► Decide to reach freedom through releasing

**24 October 2020**

**hai:** How do you use Step Two?

**Feng:** Step Two is simply a decision: decide on freedom, decide to use this releasing process to reach freedom. Sometimes you hesitate. You think, “Should I release this or not?” Or you want to use another method, such as awareness, or self-enquiry. As long as you haven't decided to use the releasing process to reach freedom, you'll keep wavering like this, and you won't be able to keep going.

Wanting freedom and deciding on freedom are both things you can simply do. *just want it more, just make the decision.*

When a wanting control or wanting approval is running you, and you're sure that releasing it is better than leaving it inside, it will come up. For example, say you want to go and play a game right now. There's a wanting control in it, running you. If you feel that releasing this wanting control matters more than controlling, you've achieved Step One, and it will come up for you to release.

Wanting control and approval = wanting the world — that is, going in the direction of limitation. There's no freedom to be had in the direction of limitation.

## 02

### ► If you have really decided on freedom, you'll do it now; if you have really decided on freedom, you won't stop until you get there

**23 October 2020**

**Feng:** Step One is wanting freedom; Step Two is deciding on freedom. If you've only decided to feel good, or to reach some material goal, you'll stop once you've got it. If you decide on freedom, you won't stop until you reach freedom. Because whatever you want and decide on, you'll take yourself there. In my second week of releasing I saw the Six Steps. I directly “knew” what the Six Steps are, and was certain they can take people all the way to the end.

---

**Feng:** Deciding on freedom isn't a prayer, and it isn't autosuggestion. A decision is a decision. It's like deciding right now to get up from your seat and walk to the door. Once you've decided, you do it straight away.
