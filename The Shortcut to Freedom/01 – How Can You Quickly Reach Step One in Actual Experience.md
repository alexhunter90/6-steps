# How Can You Quickly Reach Step One in Actual Experience?

*Original: [如何可以快速经验性地达成第一步？](../chinese/%E5%85%AC%E4%BC%97%E5%8F%B7%E8%87%AA%E7%94%B1%E7%9A%84%E6%8D%B7%E5%BE%84/%E5%A6%82%E4%BD%95%E5%8F%AF%E4%BB%A5%E5%BF%AB%E9%80%9F%E7%BB%8F%E9%AA%8C%E6%80%A7%E5%9C%B0%E8%BE%BE%E6%88%90%E7%AC%AC%E4%B8%80%E6%AD%A5%EF%BC%9F.md) · English translation by Claude (Anthropic, Opus 5.5), November 2026 · [Back to English contents](../README.md)*

25 September 2021 · The Shortcut to Freedom
(Backup source: 360doc.com)

**Tiancheng:**
Feng, how can you quickly reach Step One in actual experience?

**Feng:**
Follow the Six Steps all the way through.

**Feng:**
Of the Six Steps, the one people most easily overlook is Step Six.

**Feng:**
But Step Six is exactly the key to achieving Step One.

**Feng:**
Because, in your actual experience, you are looking for happiness in the direction of the world.

**Feng:**
So you need to put into practice Lester's favourite teaching:

**Feng:**
*get everything by releasing only*

**Feng:**
Every time you want something, get it through releasing.

**Feng:**
That gain will make you want to release even more.

**Feng:**
Keep confirming your *gain*, and it will keep you releasing constantly —

**Feng:**
until you find that your goals are being achieved one after another, and you still aren't happy.

**Feng:**
Then you will “discover”, in your actual experience, that nothing at all can bring you happiness.

**Feng:**
Instantly, you will become aware of the one and only source of happiness.

**Feng:**
At that moment, the direction of flow changes immediately —

**Feng:**
a complete change in the direction your life flows.

**Feng:**
You have turned around completely.

**Feng:**
That is achieving Step One fully:

**Feng:**
wanting freedom more than wanting the world.

**Feng:**
After that, just as Lester said, you keep falling into the remaining five steps.

> **Translator's notes**
> - “In actual experience” translates 经验性地 / 经验层面: knowing something by living it, not just understanding it intellectually. It is used throughout this collection.
> - “Gain” (Feng uses the English word) means a benefit or result you got from releasing. To “confirm your gain” is to notice, and acknowledge to yourself, that a good result came from releasing.
> - “Turned around” (调转了方向) means turning your direction away from the world and inward, towards the Self, as in Lester's description of Step One.
