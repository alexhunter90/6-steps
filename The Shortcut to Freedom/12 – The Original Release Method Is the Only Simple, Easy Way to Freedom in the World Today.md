## Talk 1 — 20 November 2020

**Feng:**
Something else I've seen concerns the Release Method. Every age has the method best suited to it.

And the Release Method is the only method in this age by which freedom can be reached simply and practicably.

You read that right — the only one. Before it, the fastest method was Kriya Yoga.

At the time I thought, “Is that really so?” Then I looked through the workbook and found that Lester says it very clearly.

This is the only one.

> **Lester's own English, exactly as it appears in the original file:**
>
> "You will discover that the Sedona Method is the only easy "how to" that the world has today that is effective and proven very quickly by your experience.
>
> Everyone in his or her every act is looking for this ulti-mate state that inheres in us now.  But they are looking for it where it is not -- in the materiality of the world.  This "how to" will enable you to get whatever it is you wish and help you get to the top state. What the world needs now is love more love which is yours now and is proven by your experience in the use of the Sedona Method,  which simply releases the non-love and al-lows your natural existing love to express.
>
> Your life will get healthier, happier, and richer.  Good luck and best wishes for your success. "
>
> Lester

> **Translator's note:** After the English, the original gives a Chinese translation of its first sentence only. That sentence says the same as Lester's English: “You will discover that the Sedona Method is the only simple ‘how to’ method in the world today that is effective, and this will very quickly be proven by your experience.”

## Talk 2 — 26 November 2020

**Student:** Feng, last time you said you knew 112 methods for reaching freedom.

**Feng:** Yes.

**Student:** Before the Release Method appeared, Kriya Yoga was the fastest.

**Feng:** Mm. The Release Method is faster — much faster.

**Student:** How long did Yogananda's woman disciple take to merge into the Infinite by practising Kriya Yoga?

**Feng:** According to Kriyananda, she too took many years.

**Student:** Mm. How did you know these 112 methods?

**Feng:** I knew them from samadhi — all 112.

**Student:** Directly knew them?

**Feng:** Mm. As to the methods themselves, I knew them directly. Then, following inner guidance, I found suitable language in the classic texts to express them.

**Student:** So a master knows all the methods?

**Feng:** Yes. A master knows everything — all-knowing, all-powerful, everywhere present.

**Student:** The teachings the Buddha and Jesus gave in their lifetimes — were those the fastest methods for the people of their time?

**Feng:** Mm — the methods best suited to the nature of the people of that time.

**Student:** So their methods don't suit the present age?

**Feng:** If you use their methods, you can't reach freedom quickly in the present age.

In seventy years humanity will evolve and there will be better methods, because by then people will be able to sense the subtle nature of creation and come into direct contact with God's thought and the particles of life.

**Student:** Do the 112 methods you know include the better methods of seventy years from now?

**Feng:** Mm. Those 112 are all the possible paths.

## Talk 3 — 2 January 2021

**Feng:**
With the 112 methods, it's only a question of which suits the age. In the present age, I recommend only the Release Method.

If you look at the workbook, you'll find Lester said:

“You will find that this method is the only simple, easy method in the world today.”

After going free, Kam told you, “It is the most highest method possible.”

They're not joking with you. A master will never fool you.
